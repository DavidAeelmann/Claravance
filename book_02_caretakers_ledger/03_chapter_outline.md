# 03 — Fair-Play Clue Matrix & 25-Chapter Blueprint (*The Caretaker's Ledger*)

**Target Manuscript Length:** ~48,000–52,000 words across 25 chapters (~1,750–2,200 words per chapter; Chapter 1 locked verbatim at 1,306 words from Book 1's back-matter sneak peek).
**POV & Tense Architecture:**
- **Chapters 1–16 & 19–25:** Clara Vance — **1st-Person Present Tense (`I`)**.
- **Chapters 17–18 (Act IIb Perspective Flip):** Maya Vance & Hollis Greer — **3rd-Person Limited Past Tense (`she` / `he`)**, revealing the Tuesday night blizzard collapse, the ledger theft, and how Maya baited the Boston contract.

## 2. 25-Chapter Beat-by-Beat Blueprint

### Act I: Locked In at Blackthorne Lodge (Chapters 1–6)

#### Chapter 1 (LOCKED VERBATIM — 1,306 Words)
- **POV & Tense:** Clara Vance (1st-Person Present).
- **Setting:** Unplowed Northeast Kingdom logging road, Blackthorne iron gates, front porch, and Great Hall (Wednesday dusk, Day 1).
- **Micro-Goal & Conflict:** Clara arrives with Sloane Mercer-Hall's $45,000 contract and brass skeleton key to verify the guest ledger bearing her missing sister Maya's handwriting (*Don't trust him.*).
- **Clues Planted/Triggered:** **Clue #1** (Fresh mud-terrain truck ruts under gate; padlocked carriage barn) & **Clue #2** (Hearth glowing with lamp kerosene and burnt bond paper; sliced guest ledger spine with left-slanting blue `m` scrap and `ROOM 4 — ATTIC EAST` brass tag on wet glue).
- **Page-Turn Cliffhanger:** A heavy floorboard groans across the third-floor attic rafters overhead, and a key turns in the upper stairwell lock, sealing Clara inside the hall.

#### Chapter 2
- **POV & Tense:** Clara Vance (1st-Person Present).
- **Setting:** Great Hall, Grand Staircase landing, and Hall Mudroom (Wednesday night, 5:15 PM).
- **Micro-Goal & Conflict:** Locked inside the dim hall with someone above her on the third floor, Clara tests the perimeter doors and races up the main stairs to check the locked third-floor fire door before the watcher descends.
- **Clues Planted/Triggered:** **Clue #4** (Finds Arthur Vane's custom rubber-ferruled orthopedic gout cane and dry shearling boots still in the mudroom rack) & **Clue #5** (Spots a three-pad right-hand oil smudge—missing the ring fingertip—on the upper stairwell deadbolt plate).
- **Page-Turn Cliffhanger:** As Clara presses her ear to the third-floor fire door, the house generator cuts out—and a mechanical dumbwaiter cable hisses downward inside the wall right beside her shoulder.

#### Chapter 3
- **POV & Tense:** Clara Vance (1st-Person Present).
- **Setting:** Commercial Kitchen, Butler's Pantry dumbwaiter, and Arthur's Walnut Study (Wednesday night, 6:00 PM).
- **Micro-Goal & Conflict:** Tracking the dumbwaiter drop into the dark butler's pantry, Clara searches Arthur's study for a house floor plan and recovers charred fragments from the fireplace ash trap before the intruder circles down the service stairs.
- **Clues Planted/Triggered:** **Clue #2 expansion** (Finds a half-burned Vane Biotech clinical payout voucher in the hearth ash-trap and realizes the `ROOM 4` brass tag glue matches an uncapped hide-glue bottle hidden inside the pantry dumbwaiter car—meaning two different people were in the hall minutes before she arrived).
- **Page-Turn Cliffhanger:** A gloved fist slams against the frosted kitchen porch glass from outside, and a man in a snow-crusted parka aims a double-barreled shotgun through the pane: *"Put the brass key on the butcher block, lady, or I blow the latch off."*

#### Chapter 4
- **POV & Tense:** Clara Vance (1st-Person Present).
- **Setting:** Kitchen threshold & Exterior Woodshed / Snowmobile Bay (Wednesday night, 7:15 PM).
- **Micro-Goal & Conflict:** Caretaker **Hollis Greer** claims he locked the attic stairs to trap a squatter and insists the Vanes vanished on the snowmobiles Tuesday night; Clara feigns compliance as a routine Boston estate clerk to inspect the three snowmobiles by the woodshed.
- **Clues Planted/Triggered:** **Clue #3** (Clara checks the three Arctic Cat snowmobiles under Hollis's flashlight: ignition switches are `ON`, but there are no melted exhaust troughs in Tuesday's snow, engine-hour meters match last month's log, and raw gasoline smells fresh around the brass fuel petcocks—the tanks were siphoned, not idled dry).
- **Page-Turn Cliffhanger:** Hollis's right glove slips off as he grabs the woodshed padlock—exposing the scarred stump of his missing right ring fingertip—just as Clara spots a drop of fresh dark-blue fountain ink frozen onto the snow beside the stone icehouse.

#### Chapter 5
- **POV & Tense:** Clara Vance (1st-Person Present).
- **Setting:** Timber Carriage Barn & Spruce Turnout (Wednesday night, 9:00 PM).
- **Micro-Goal & Conflict:** Ordered by Hollis to retrieve her inventory forms from her Civic down at the gate while he watches from the porch, Clara uses the dark timber blind spot to shim the padlocked carriage barn doors.
- **Clues Planted/Triggered:** **Clue #1 triggered** (Inside the barn sits Hollis's warm Ford F-250 on mud-terrain tires; in the truck bed under a tarp sits a woman's Moncler winter coat—Celeste Vane's—and a crate of shredded *VaneCor* pediatric autopsy transfers).
- **Page-Turn Cliffhanger:** Reaching her Civic outside the iron gates to grab her spare battery pack, Clara finds her hood warm, the distributor wire clipped, and her tire chains sliced through with bolt cutters.

#### Chapter 6
- **POV & Tense:** Clara Vance (1st-Person Present).
- **Setting:** Second-Floor Guest Room & Sub-Basement Boiler Vault (Wednesday night, 11:30 PM).
- **Micro-Goal & Conflict:** Stranded on the mountain with a sabotaged car and zero cell bars, Clara sets a thread tamper-trap across her guest bedroom door and slips down to the sub-basement boiler room after Hollis locks in for the night.
- **Clues Planted/Triggered:** **Clue #6** (Clara logs the boiler fuel gauge: 18 gallons burned in 48 hours despite freezing main rooms, with a manual brass damper open full-throttle toward the **East Attic riser**).
- **Page-Turn Cliffhanger:** Behind the furnace, the iron ash-pit door clanks open from the outside coal chute, and a freezing draft carries a whispered voice through the dark: *"Clara? Don't turn your flashlight on."* *(Act I Break)*

### Act IIa: The Audit of Room 4 & The Frozen Vault (Chapters 7–13)
