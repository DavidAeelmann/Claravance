## 2. Setting & Architectural Blueprint: Blackthorne Lodge (Northeast Kingdom, Vermont)
Located in northern Essex/Orleans County ("Granite Ridge"), three miles up a private unplowed logging road beyond the county snowplow turnaround. Surrounded by dense black spruce and sheer granite ledges.
- **Perimeter & Outbuildings:**
  - **Wrought-Iron Gates & Spruce Turnout:** Forged iron spears between fieldstone pillars. Clara's Civic is hidden in the spruce turnout outside. Fresh mud-terrain waffle-tread pickup ruts cut through the snow *into* the estate (no outbound tracks).
  - **Timber Carriage Barn (50 paces behind main house):** Double timber doors padlocked from the *outside* with a heavy brass padlock. Conceals a matte-charcoal Ford F-250 pickup with warm engine block, mud-terrain tires, and a hidden crate of shredded clinical transport manifests.
  - **Woodshed & Snowmobile Bay:** Three Arctic Cat snowmobiles sitting outside in the drift with ignition keys turned to `ON` and fuel tanks bone-dry—except forensic siphon smell and a clean brass drain cock on the reserve line prove the tanks were drained into jerrycans, not idled dry over hours.
  - **Stone Icehouse & Coal Chute:** Half-buried granite icehouse behind the kitchen wing connected to the lodge's sub-basement boiler room via a brick coal-and-ash chute.
- **First Floor (Main Level):**
  - **Covered Hemlock Porch & Oak Entry Door:** Oversized iron mortise lock (freshly oiled wards) opened by Clara's brass skeleton key.
  - **Great Hall:** High clerestory windows near the rafters, mounted stag heads, flagstone floor, massive fieldstone hearth (glowing birch ash, lamp kerosene residue, charred bond paper), and slanted 19th-century oak reception lectern holding the maroon-leather **Guest Ledger**.
  - **Arthur's Walnut Study:** Adjacent to the great hall; wall safe behind topographical survey maps; locked mahogany apothecary cabinet; brass desk weight matched to the one pinning the torn ledger scrap.
  - **Commercial Kitchen & Butler's Pantry:** Cast-iron radiator heating system (fed by the basement dual-fuel boiler), walk-in cold larder, and service dumbwaiter/pipe chase running vertically to the second-floor suites and third-floor East Attic.
- **Second Floor (Family & Guest Wing):**
  - **Master Suite (Arthur & Celeste Vane):** Cedar armoire, Celeste's staged winter packing, and bathroom medicine safe containing compounded cardiac vials (*VaneCor-9* off-label trial lot).
  - **West Gallery & Caretaker's Office:** Property maintenance logs, generator load charts, and hardwired intercom panel.
- **Third Floor (The Locked Attic Level):**
  - **Upper Stairwell Fire Door:** Heavy oak-and-iron door at the top of the second-floor landing with a mortise deadbolt (locked from the *inside* at the end of Chapter 1).
  - **Room 4 — Attic East:** Former live-in nurse/archivist quarters under the steep east gable. Contains a hidden false ceiling louver above the radiator riser, a draftsman's table with dark-blue fountain ink, and a cold-storage medical lockbox.
- **Sub-Basement:**
  - **Boiler & Generator Vault:** Cast-iron oil/kerosene furnace, backup diesel tank, ash-pit cleanout door, and the brick coal chute leading to the exterior icehouse.

## 3. Cast Dossiers, Financial/Criminal Motives & Handedness/Physical Anchors

1. **Maya Vance (27 — Clara's Younger Sister, aka "Mara Abbott"):**
   - **Physical & Forensic Anchors:** **Left-handed** with a distinctive **slanted, left-leaning cursive** and a habit of looping the lowercase `m` with a sharp downward tail; uses dark blue waterproof archival ink (`Noodler's Prussian Blue`); slight childhood scar on her right collarbone; smells of pine turpentine and wool.
   - **Backstory & Motive:** Six years ago, as a 21-year-old biostatistics intern in Burlington, Maya discovered that *Vane Biotech* falsified fatal arrhythmia data on their flagship pediatric compound (*VaneCor*) by burying three patient deaths under rural "accidental hypothermia" autopsies. When Vane's fixers came after her, Maya vanished off-grid to protect herself and a bankrupt Clara. Two months ago, using forged credentials as seasonal estate archivist "Mara Abbott," Maya infiltrated Blackthorne Lodge to steal Arthur Vane's original unredacted paper ledger (the **Caretaker's Ledger** of hush-money annuities paid to silenced rural coroners and families).

2. **Arthur Vane (62 — Pharmaceutical Heir & Patriarch):**
   - **Physical & Forensic Anchors:** Right-handed; silver beard, severe gout in his left big toe (requires a custom orthopedic cane with a rubber ferrule and cannot walk more than fifty yards in deep snow without it); smokes imported clove cigarillos.
   - **Status & Motive:** **Already dead before Chapter 1 begins** (died Tuesday night during the blizzard—not on a snowmobile, but inside the lodge study from an acute cardiac arrest triggered during a confrontation over the ledger, concealed in the stone icehouse packed in dry salt-ice so his death timestamp can be manipulated until Friday's trust-vesting deadline). If Arthur is declared dead *before* Friday at 5:00 PM EST, a **$38,000,000 survivorship clawback** reverts his voting shares to Mercer-Hall Fiduciary's institutional creditors instead of his wife Celeste.

3. **Hollis Greer (49 — The Live-In Winter Caretaker):**
   - **Physical & Forensic Anchors:** **Right-handed**, missing the distal tip of his right ring finger (leaves a distinct three-point smudge on brass hardware and oiled locks); wears size-11 Vibram lug-sole Sorel boots (the heavy boot groan on the third-floor rafters at the end of Chapter 1); smells of lamp kerosene, pine resin, and wintergreen smokeless tobacco.
   - **Motive & Role:** Former private security contractor and long-time "cleaner" on Vane's off-book payroll. Hollis's name is inside the *Caretaker's Ledger* as the bagman who staged a coroner's hypothermia death in 2020. On Tuesday night, when Maya ("Mara") tried to photograph the ledger during the blizzard and Arthur suffered a fatal cardiac collapse, Hollis and Celeste staged the three-snowmobile disappearance to stall inquiries until Friday at 5:00 PM—while Hollis hunted Maya through the snowbound estate after realizing "Mara" was still hiding on the ridge.

4. **Celeste Vane (36 — Arthur's Second Wife):**
   - **Physical & Forensic Anchors:** Right-handed; former clinical trial coordinator; wears cashmere turtlenecks and compounded lavender cuticle oil; severe raynaud's circulation in her fingers.
   - **Motive & Role:** Stands to inherit **$38,000,000** exclusively if Arthur is proved alive through Friday at 5:00 PM (or if his body is found after the deadline in an "accidental" snowmobile exposure death). She is hiding alive inside the estate's West Wing / Carriage Barn axis with Hollis, helping stage the disappearance while secretly plotting to pin both Arthur's death and the burned clinical records on Hollis and Maya.

5. **Sloane Mercer-Hall (44 — Boston Fiduciary Counsel):**
   - **Motive & Role:** Her fiduciary firm manages the $38M institutional bond backing *Vane Biotech*. If Arthur Vane is proved dead *before* Friday at 5:00 PM, the $38M stays with Sloane's institutional bondholders instead of vesting to Celeste. That is why Sloane needs Arthur's corpse found before Friday—and why, after receiving an anonymous dead-drop scan of the Blackthorne guest ledger two days ago (sent by Maya) and recognizing Maya's handwriting from Clara's background file, Sloane used it as surgical leverage to send Clara up the mountain. Crucially, Sloane wants the *Caretaker's Ledger* suppressed rather than made public, since exposing *VaneCor* coroner bribes would crater the stock and trigger federal clawbacks against her firm ("produce the body for the bondholders, bury the ledger for the board").

6. **Warden / Trooper Galen Vance-Ross ("Deputy Enoch Pike", 52 — Local Essex County Deputy / Snowplow Contractor):**
   - **Physical & Forensic Anchors:** Drives the heavy county plow/pickup; chews cinnamon toothpicks; takes winter retainer envelopes from Hollis to keep the private road listed as "impassable" until Friday night.
