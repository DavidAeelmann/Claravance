## 2. Setting & Architectural Blueprint: Cragmont Lodge (Northeast Kingdom, Vermont)
Located in northern Essex/Orleans County ("Granite Ridge"), three miles up a private unplowed logging road beyond the county snowplow turnaround. Surrounded by dense black spruce and sheer granite ledges.
- **Perimeter & Outbuildings:**
  - **Wrought-Iron Gates & Spruce Turnout:** Forged iron spears between fieldstone pillars. Tessa's Civic is hidden in the spruce turnout outside. Fresh mud-terrain waffle-tread pickup ruts cut through the snow *into* the estate (no outbound tracks).
  - **Timber Carriage Barn (50 paces behind main house):** Double timber doors padlocked from the *outside* with a heavy brass padlock. Conceals a matte-charcoal Ford F-250 pickup with warm engine block, mud-terrain tires, and a hidden crate of shredded clinical transport manifests.
  - **Woodshed & Snowmobile Bay:** Three Arctic Cat snowmobiles sitting outside in the drift with ignition keys turned to `ON` and fuel tanks bone-dry—except forensic siphon smell and a clean brass drain cock on the reserve line prove the tanks were drained into jerrycans, not idled dry over hours.
  - **Stone Icehouse & Coal Chute:** Half-buried granite icehouse behind the kitchen wing connected to the lodge's sub-basement boiler room via a brick coal-and-ash chute.
- **First Floor (Main Level):**
  - **Covered Hemlock Porch & Oak Entry Door:** Oversized iron mortise lock (freshly oiled wards) opened by Tessa's brass skeleton key.
  - **Great Hall:** High clerestory windows near the rafters, mounted stag heads, flagstone floor, massive fieldstone hearth (glowing birch ash, lamp kerosene residue, charred bond paper), and slanted 19th-century oak reception lectern holding the maroon-leather **Guest Ledger**.
  - **Arthur's Walnut Study:** Adjacent to the great hall; wall safe behind topographical survey maps; locked mahogany apothecary cabinet; brass desk weight matched to the one pinning the torn ledger scrap.
  - **Commercial Kitchen & Butler's Pantry:** Cast-iron radiator heating system (fed by the basement dual-fuel boiler), walk-in cold larder, and service dumbwaiter/pipe chase running vertically to the second-floor suites and third-floor East Attic.
- **Second Floor (Family & Guest Wing):**
  - **Master Suite (Arthur & Simone Cutler):** Cedar armoire, Simone's staged winter packing, and bathroom medicine safe containing compounded cardiac vials (*CutlerCor-9* off-label trial lot).
  - **West Gallery & Caretaker's Office:** Property maintenance logs, generator load charts, and hardwired intercom panel.
- **Third Floor (The Locked Attic Level):**
  - **Upper Stairwell Fire Door:** Heavy oak-and-iron door at the top of the second-floor landing with a mortise deadbolt (locked from the *inside* at the end of Chapter 1).
  - **Room 4 — Attic East:** Former live-in nurse/archivist quarters under the steep east gable. Contains a hidden false ceiling louver above the radiator riser, a draftsman's table with dark-blue fountain ink, and a cold-storage medical lockbox.
- **Sub-Basement:**
  - **Boiler & Generator Vault:** Cast-iron oil/kerosene furnace, backup diesel tank, ash-pit cleanout door, and the brick coal chute leading to the exterior icehouse.

## 3. Cast Dossiers, Financial/Criminal Motives & Handedness/Physical Anchors

1. **Nina Calder (27 — Tessa's Younger Sister, aka "Lena Talbot"):**
   - **Physical & Forensic Anchors:** **Left-handed** with a distinctive **slanted, left-leaning cursive** and a habit of looping the lowercase `m` with a sharp downward tail; uses dark blue waterproof archival ink (`Noodler's Prussian Blue`); slight childhood scar on her right collarbone; smells of pine turpentine and wool.
   - **Backstory & Motive:** Six years ago, as a 21-year-old biostatistics intern in Burlington, Nina discovered that *Cutler Biotech* falsified fatal arrhythmia data on their flagship pediatric compound (*CutlerCor*) by burying three patient deaths under rural "accidental hypothermia" autopsies. When Cutler's fixers came after her, Nina vanished off-grid to protect herself and a bankrupt Tessa. Two months ago, using forged credentials as seasonal estate archivist "Lena Talbot," Nina infiltrated Cragmont Lodge to steal Arthur Cutler's original unredacted paper ledger (the **Caretaker's Ledger** of hush-money annuities paid to silenced rural coroners and families).

2. **Arthur Cutler (62 — Pharmaceutical Heir & Patriarch):**
   - **Physical & Forensic Anchors:** Right-handed; silver beard, severe gout in his left big toe (requires a custom orthopedic cane with a rubber ferrule and cannot walk more than fifty yards in deep snow without it); smokes imported clove cigarillos.
   - **Status & Motive:** **Already dead before Chapter 1 begins** (died Tuesday night during the blizzard—not on a snowmobile, but inside the lodge study from an acute cardiac arrest triggered during a confrontation over the ledger, concealed in the stone icehouse packed in dry salt-ice so his death timestamp can be manipulated until Friday's trust-vesting deadline). If Arthur is declared dead *before* Friday at 5:00 PM EST, a **$38,000,000 survivorship clawback** reverts his voting shares to Kiefer-Hall Fiduciary's institutional creditors instead of his wife Simone.

3. **Hollis Greer (49 — The Live-In Winter Caretaker):**
   - **Physical & Forensic Anchors:** **Right-handed**, missing the distal tip of his right ring finger (leaves a distinct three-point smudge on brass hardware and oiled locks); wears size-11 Vibram lug-sole Sorel boots (the heavy boot groan on the third-floor rafters at the end of Chapter 1); smells of lamp kerosene, pine resin, and wintergreen smokeless tobacco.
   - **Motive & Role:** Former private security contractor and long-time "cleaner" on Cutler's off-book payroll. Hollis's name is inside the *Caretaker's Ledger* as the bagman who staged a coroner's hypothermia death in 2020. On Tuesday night, when Nina ("Lena") tried to photograph the ledger during the blizzard and Arthur suffered a fatal cardiac collapse, Hollis and Simone staged the three-snowmobile disappearance to stall inquiries until Friday at 5:00 PM—while Hollis hunted Nina through the snowbound estate after realizing "Lena" was still hiding on the ridge.

4. **Simone Cutler (36 — Arthur's Second Wife):**
   - **Physical & Forensic Anchors:** Right-handed; former clinical trial coordinator; wears cashmere turtlenecks and compounded lavender cuticle oil; severe raynaud's circulation in her fingers.
   - **Motive & Role:** Stands to inherit **$38,000,000** exclusively if Arthur is proved alive through Friday at 5:00 PM (or if his body is found after the deadline in an "accidental" snowmobile exposure death). She is hiding alive inside the estate's West Wing / Carriage Barn axis with Hollis, helping stage the disappearance while secretly plotting to pin both Arthur's death and the burned clinical records on Hollis and Nina.

5. **Margot Kiefer-Hall (44 — Boston Fiduciary Counsel):**
   - **Motive & Role:** Her fiduciary firm manages the $38M institutional bond backing *Cutler Biotech*. If Arthur Cutler is proved dead *before* Friday at 5:00 PM, the $38M stays with Margot's institutional bondholders instead of vesting to Simone. When the anonymous overnight fax arrived at Kiefer-Hall Tuesday night showing a cropped corner of Cragmont Lodge's guest ledger (*Don't trust him.*), Margot assumed her missing archivist "Lena Talbot" had sent it before vanishing, and taped it to the back of the Cragmont photo as a standard investigative briefing exhibit. Only when Tessa flipped the photo over at Becky's Diner and froze dead silent did Margot realize the handwriting hit a personal nerve—and let Tessa's silence recruit her. Crucially, Margot wants the *Caretaker's Ledger* suppressed rather than made public, since exposing *CutlerCor* coroner bribes would crater the stock and trigger federal clawbacks against her firm ("produce the body for the bondholders, bury the ledger for the board").

6. **Warden / Trooper Galen Calder-Ross ("Deputy Enoch Pike", 52 — Local Essex County Deputy / Snowplow Contractor):**
   - **Physical & Forensic Anchors:** Drives the heavy county plow/pickup; chews cinnamon toothpicks; takes winter retainer envelopes from Hollis to keep the private road listed as "impassable" until Friday night.
