## 1. Tessa Calder (Protagonist / First-Person POV Narrator)

- **Name and Aliases:** Tessa Calder (`Ms. Calder`, `"Portland cleaner"` to Cole Halstead).
- **Physical Description:**
  - Not heavily self-described by facial features (tired face reflected in ocean glass, Ch. 1; damp hair); physically capable, compact enough to squeeze through a narrow 1970s dumbwaiter utility chase `"barely wider than my shoulders"` / walk-in pantry `"barely wider than my hips"` (Ch. 8, 11).
  - *Wardrobe:* Waxed jacket, flannel shirt, jeans with a coin/watch pocket, thick wool socks, laced boots (Ch. 1–14), dry spare sneakers (Ch. 15–22), dark wool sweater pulled over wet flannel (Ch. 20–22).
  - *Injuries Sustained in Book 1:*
    - Scraped right palm from catching herself on raw granite cliff steps (Ch. 16, line 41; torn open again in Ch. 18, line 53 and Ch. 19, line 71).
    - Raw laceration circling her left wrist above the palm from sawing her marine nylon zip tie against a rusted oyster knife blade (Ch. 17, line 83; prevents her from gripping the iron ladder with her left hand in Ch. 19 & 21; closed with **three neat stitches** by a Knox County paramedic in Ch. 22, line 91; leaves a **pale pink seam** under her wool sweater cuff four weeks later in Ch. 23, line 9).
    - Bruised left knee from Lydia kicking her backward off the vestibule coat bench (Ch. 22, line 9; still aches in the December cold in Ch. 23, line 9).
- **Age:** Not explicitly stated (implied early-to-mid 30s; had an established career as a commercial fraud investigator before her 2023 termination, and lived in a Burlington apartment six years ago when her younger sister Nina disappeared).
- **Relationships:**
  - **Nina Calder (Younger Sister):** Disappeared in northern Vermont six years ago (2020); shared an apartment with Tessa in Burlington before vanishing (Ch. 11, line 5). A Vermont sheriff's deputy closed Nina's white binder in three weeks without checking dates (Ch. 2, 5, 20).
  - **Samira Patel (Professional Contact/Ally):** Marine-claims investigator in Portland with read-only access to the Atlantic Marine Underwriters' cross-border port-clearance watch (Ch. 5, 10, 14, 16).
  - **Graham Kiefer (Employer / Reluctant Co-Target):** Hires Tessa via a blind broker to pack and log his missing wife's estate; later forced into an uneasy tactical alliance when both are targeted by Lydia and Cole, though Tessa ultimately refuses his bribe and turns in his forgery ledger (Ch. 1–22).
  - **Maren Lindqvist (Local Informant / Rescuer):** Salt Point oyster harvester who warns Tessa and brings the Maine State Police to save her (Ch. 3, 5, 9, 18, 22, 23).
  - **Margot Kiefer-Hall (Escrow Agent / Book 2 Recruiter):** Releases Tessa's $13,500 completion/storm check and recruits her in the epilogue using Nina's handwriting (Ch. 23).
- **Occupation and Relevant Expertise:**
  - Former commercial fraud claims investigator at **Harbor Mutual** in Portland (terminated and blacklisted in 2023 for refusing to sign off on a fraudulent Commercial Street fire manifest without chain-of-custody, even after a senior partner offered her a quiet raise to sign it, Ch. 1, 15, 20).
  - Currently working as an independent estate inventory/pack-out contractor while her private investigator surety bond is suspended due to an unpaid defamation judgment; her PI bond is officially restored by the Maine Department of Public Safety on Monday, December 7, 2026 (Ch. 23, line 5).
  - *Investigative & Forensic Skills:* Strict chain-of-custody discipline (shooting timestamped flash-off photos before touching items, using nitrile gloves and acid-free tissue, packaging trace swabs in heavy foil inside a knotted glove finger, setting telltale lining threads across duffel zipper teeth); single-locked handcuff shimming/picking with an awl (Ch. 20); familiarity with marine loss claims, low-voltage alarm logs, and fiduciary/trust documentation.
- **Known History & Financial Status:**
  - Sued for **$42,000** in a retaliatory defamation suit after her 2023 Harbor Mutual firing; owes a remaining balance of **$18,412** to the Cumberland County clerk (faces a Monday bank levy and eviction from her Portland studio apartment; has **$400 and change** in her checking account at the start of Ch. 1).
  - Drives a rusted silver **2014 Honda Civic** (12 years old in 2026) with a rattling muffler shield and a burned-out right brake lamp (repaired by Ch. 23).
- **Personality, Motivations & Behavioral Patterns:**
  - Pragmatic, financially cornered, hyper-observant, and constitutionally incapable of ignoring a falsified record ("stubborn enough not to walk past a six-minute Zone 4 dropout or a dry life vest," Ch. 15, line 47).
  - Driven at first by survival ($15,000 cash contract to stop Monday's court levy), then by self-defense against a murder frame, and ultimately by an unshakeable moral refusal to become "a paid signature" (linked directly to the trauma of her sister Nina's brushed-aside disappearance).
- **Important Decisions:**
  1. Refuses to sign Graham's $50,000 NDA until he writes and initials a margin exception allowing timestamped estate-verification photographs (Ch. 1, lines 75–77).
  2. Logs and investigates the studio, sunroom, and bathroom anomalies despite the $50,000 breach penalty (Ch. 2, 4, 7).
  3. Sends Lydia's passport info to Samira Patel for a cross-border port-clearance watch (Ch. 5, line 59).
  4. Spots the chalky swirl in her poured bourbon glass and swaps glasses with Graham while he fetches ice (Ch. 10, lines 45–47).
  5. Enters the hidden dumbwaiter shaft at 2:30 AM and pries open the `ARCHIVE` cabinet in Zone 4 (Ch. 11–12).
  6. Saws free of her zip tie in the boathouse while intentionally baiting Lydia into confessing over the live `BOATHOUSE_LINT` PoE microphone puck (Ch. 17).
  7. Gouges `CALL STATE POLICE — FRAME AT KIEFER HOUSE` onto Maren's tide strip and jams it behind Maren's sorting-shed latch (Ch. 18, line 45).
  8. Pulls the dumbwaiter pulley locking pin to drop the pig-iron counterweight as a diversion (Ch. 19, lines 69–75).
  9. **Defining Moral Choice:** Rejects Graham's offer of $68,412 ($18,412 judgment payoff + $50,000 fee + bond restoration) over the lit kitchen gas stove, saves his forgery packet from the flame at knifepoint, and hands it over to Detective Sergeant Ward (Ch. 20, lines 49–69; Ch. 22, lines 75–85).
- **First / Last Appearance:** Chapter 1 (line 1) to Chapter 23 (line 97) — appears in every chapter.

## 2. Graham Kiefer

- **Name and Aliases:** Graham Kiefer (`Mr. Kiefer`, `G.K.`).
- **Physical Description:**
  - Thinner and more gaunt than in his spring magazine profile; pale gray eyes with bruised-looking hollows underneath; gray stubble when sleep-deprived (Ch. 13); slight tremor in his wrist under stress (Ch. 3, line 89; Ch. 14, line 42).

- **Occupation and Relevant Expertise:**
  - Founder and head of **Kiefer Soundworks LLC** (based in Cambridge, Massachusetts, with his acoustic darkroom and prototype servers in Basement Zone 4 at Salt Point).
  - Acoustic engineer developing directional microphone arrays, wind-noise reduction filters, and noise-canceling/acoustic prototypes under Defense Department NDAs for an Arlington, Virginia defense prime buyer.
  - Zero maritime/boating competence (`"Graham doesn't know a cleat hitch from a shoelace"`, Maren in Ch. 9, line 67).
- **Relationships:**
  - **Lydia Kiefer (Wife):** Married into Lydia's wealthy coastal Maine family (her family owned the original 1970s cottage on Salt Point and established *The Lydia Kiefer Irrevocable Trust* of $5.4M plus separate Cayman offshore accounts).
  - **Lydia's Brother in Zurich (Brother-in-Law):** Estranged from Lydia since their father's death; pressuring Boston trustees at Bramwell & Shaw to freeze joint accounts.
  - **Margot Kiefer-Hall (Distant Cousin / Escrow Agent):** Managing partner at *Kiefer-Hall Fiduciary* in Boston; hadn't spoken to Graham in a decade until he retained her firm six weeks before the epilogue to hold the contractual archiving escrow (Ch. 23, line 41).
  - **Deputy Cole Halstead (Rival / Antagonist):** Grew up with Lydia on Salt Point and openly resents Graham's "Boston money" and Cambridge background (Ch. 5, 8, 15, 21).
- **Recurring Physical Behaviors & Speech Patterns:**
  - Compulsively squares paper and fabrics flush with wood or stone edges (squares the $5,000 cash envelope to the maple table wood grain, Ch. 1, line 69; folds a damp dish towel into a neat rectangle flush with the limestone counter edge, Ch. 6, line 87; wipes the counter where Tessa's mug sat, Ch. 3, line 95).
  - Automatically reaches for the heavier front-left cut-crystal rocks glass (with a thumb-worn etched rim) when pouring evening bourbon (Ch. 10, line 15 & 45).
  - Speaks in a controlled, dry, smooth executive cadence that only cracks when his financial insolvency or forgery is cornered.
- **Knowledge & Truth Breakdown:**
  1. **What is Objectively True:**
     - Graham embezzled **$2,400,000** over 14 months in six unauthorized draws from *The Lydia Kiefer Irrevocable Trust* ($5.4M corpus, leaving $3.0M) to fund Kiefer Soundworks' payroll and Cambridge lab lease while his Arlington defense buyout was delayed in federal export review (Ch. 10, 13, 14).
     - On **October 10, 2026** (4 days before Lydia's disappearance), Graham created a fraudulent retroactive trust-reconciliation packet, **slicing three pages out of Lydia's studio sketchbook** to practice tracing her `L.K.` initials on a light table over pencil guidelines (Ch. 2, 13, 14).
     - Graham **did not murder or attack Lydia** on October 14/15. He was sealed in Basement Zone 4 wearing noise-canceling headphones until 3:00 AM when the grid dropped (Ch. 14, line 63).
     - On **October 15**, Bramwell & Shaw served him with a formal *Notice of Default & Acceleration* giving him a 30-day civil cure window expiring **November 14** before referral to the Suffolk County DA (Ch. 10, line 63).
     - On **October 21**, Graham discovered a 6-minute dropout (`02:14:44–02:21:02 AM`) in his house acoustic array and realized the mudroom life vest returned by Cole Halstead smelled of tap water and carried a fresh lot stamp (Ch. 14, line 73). He spent the next two weeks running wind-noise reduction filters on the `02:09–02:17 AM` recordings and calculating tide/AIS charts to figure out who framed him on the `02:14:07 AM` infrared video (Ch. 12, lines 19–37).
     - On **October 21**, Graham pulled Tessa Calder's background report (`CALDER_T — VETTING`) and hired her under a $50,000 NDA as an incorruptible outsider "tripwire" to see what October 15 tampering she would flag while completing the preliminary probate ledger needed to close his Arlington buyout by November 14 (Ch. 15, lines 45–51).
     - On Wednesday night (Nov 4), Graham unknowingly drank his own zolpidem-dusted bourbon glass after Tessa swapped their tumblers, knocking him unconscious on the living-room sofa until 5:45 AM Thursday (Ch. 10, 14, 23).
     - In Ch. 20 (lines 33–63), Graham attempts to burn his October 10 forgery packet over the kitchen gas stove and offers Tessa $68,412 to let it burn; he is arrested for felony trust fraud in Ch. 22 (line 81) and awaiting a delayed bail hearing in Ch. 23 (line 9).
  2. **What Tessa Initially Believes vs. Learns:** Tessa initially suspects Graham may have murdered Lydia for the $5.4M trust and staged the house (Ch. 1–9), then realizes in Ch. 10–15 that while Graham is guilty of $2.4M in trust forgery, Lydia is alive and framing him for murder.
  3. **What Graham Claims (Falsehoods Told Early On):** Claims he has clean joint signatory authority (Ch. 3); lies to the Coast Guard and Tessa that he came upstairs at midnight on Oct 14 (Ch. 3); claims the Zone 4 motion alert during Tuesday's blackout was just a power surge (Ch. 6); claims the boathouse is under a county seal (Ch. 10).
- **First / Last Appearance:** Chapter 1 (line 25) to Chapter 22 (line 81); referenced in Chapter 23.

- **Occupation and Relevant Expertise:**
  - Botanical watercolor artist (painted coastal flora studies in her upstairs studio, e.g., *Rosa rugosa*, *Laminaria*, stopping in September 2026, Ch. 2, lines 55, 79).
  - Local heiress who grew up climbing the Salt Point ledges in her family's 1970s cottage before her father's stroke and death (Ch. 1, 5, 15).
- **Relationships:**
  - **Graham Kiefer (Husband / Target):** Discovered Graham's $2.4M forgery/draws from her trust; rather than pursuing a slow civil fraud suit or divorce, engineered a staged drowning to trigger the trust's murder/moral-turpitude forfeiture clause and destroy him (Ch. 14, 15, 17).

  1. **What is Objectively True:**
     - After catching Graham's $2.4M unauthorized draws from *The Lydia Kiefer Irrevocable Trust*, Lydia conspired with Deputy Cole Halstead to fake her own drowning on **October 14/15, 2026** and frame Graham for her murder.

     - On **October 4**, she filled a 30-tablet prescription for **Zolpidem Tartrate 10 mg (Ambien)** in Camden (later used to crush 10 tablets in the master-bath sink and dust Graham's bourbon tumbler, Ch. 7, 10, 17, 23).
     - On **Monday, October 12**, she paid Maren Lindqvist **$5,000 cash** to keep her dock lights off during the October 14 storm and asked about coastal AIS transponder blind spots (Ch. 9, lines 71–79).
     - At **02:09–02:18 AM on October 15**, Lydia and Cole used the pantry dumbwaiter hatch, staged the `02:14:07 AM` infrared camera clip (Cole in Graham's spare slicker dragging a weighted rolled canvas rug from the sunroom to the boathouse), wiped down a scratched varnished oar, left Lydia's uncapped silver Montblanc and a left-handed departure checklist in the studio, killed the main house breaker for 6 minutes (`02:14:44–02:21:02`), and rowed the skiff out at `02:18:11` (Ch. 12, 13, 15).
     - On **October 17**, her olive weekender bag passed through the **Bar Harbor–Yarmouth (Slip 4)** ferry (Ch. 1).
     - On **Sunday, November 1**, she returned to Salt Point with Cole to stage the bleached/linseed-oiled floor patch under the sunroom rug (when Cole dropped his `B-4` key behind the baseboard, Ch. 4, 17), and had Cole drop her weekender behind Graham's coats on **Monday morning, November 2** (Ch. 14, 17).
     - On **Tuesday morning, November 3 at 09:42 AST**, Lydia scanned her US Passport (`#••••••3907`) at **St. Andrews Private Marina, New Brunswick** to satisfy her father's Cayman trustee's requirement for an in-person passport verification outside the United States (Ch. 10, 17), then returned to Salt Point to hide in the basement/boathouse.
     - Once Graham hired Tessa Calder, Lydia and Cole pivoted to framing Tessa as Graham's desperate paid accomplice: stealing Tessa's spare Civic key fob, locking Tessa in Basement Zone 4 at 2:34 AM Thursday while Graham was sedated on zolpidem, planting Lydia's muddy duck boots, a bleach rag, and a pried boathouse cleat in Tessa's Civic trunk, rendering a spliced audio confession (`CALDER_CONFESSION_FINAL.wav`) uploaded from `L_KIEFER_IPAD_MINI_02` to Knox County, and attempting to stage a double murder-suicide of Graham and Tessa during Thursday's 5:15 PM high-tide surge (Ch. 14–21).
     - Captured by Tessa and Maine State Police as she tries to escape out the vestibule skylight hatch in Ch. 22; held without bail in Ch. 23.
- **First / Last Appearance:** Mentioned from Chapter 1; voice heard on storm audio in Chapter 15; first physical appearance in Chapter 16 (line 81) through Chapter 22 (line 45); referenced in Chapter 23.
