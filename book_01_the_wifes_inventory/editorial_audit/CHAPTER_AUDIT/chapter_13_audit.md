## 4. Logic and Plausibility
- **[MEDIUM] Why Would Graham Keep the Three Sliced Sketchbook Practice Sheets Inside His `OCT 14 — MASTER BACKUP` Envelope Instead of Burning/Destroying Them?**
  - *Passage (Ch. 13, lines 31–37 & 59):* Inside the red-taped `OCT 14 — MASTER BACKUP` envelope, alongside the notarized October 10 ratification packet and the raw October 15 audio transcript, Graham locked the three sliced sketchbook pages covered in his penciled practice runs of Lydia's `L.K.` initials.
  - *Plausibility Question:* Why on earth would a forger keep his **pencil practice sheets** (the literal smoking-gun proof that he traced his wife's initials on a light table!) stapled/bundled inside the same archive envelope as the forged trust schedules, rather than burning or flushing the three practice sheets on October 10 as soon as he finished tracing the six schedules?!
  - *Compare with Ch. 20 (lines 33–41):* In Ch. 20, Graham desperately tries to burn the contents of that envelope—including the three sliced sketchbook pages—over the kitchen gas stove! Why didn't he burn the practice sheets three weeks earlier on October 10?
  - *Possible Explanations:*
    1. Either Graham kept the original sliced sketchbook leaves with the real `L.K.` initials that he used *as the master template* on the light table (Wait: line 35 says `"covered in dozens of penciled practice runs of L.K., complete with light-table registration ticks along the margins"`), OR
    2. Lydia found the three sliced pages in Graham's trash/desk on October 10–11, and either Graham locked them in the steel archive cabinet so Lydia/Cole couldn't get them, or keeping his practice sheets is a major unforced error by a meticulous man.
  - *Classification:* **[MEDIUM]** character/investigative logic issue. If those three sliced pages from Lydia's sketchbook instead contained **authentic sketches/signatures by Lydia that Graham sliced out to use as light-table master templates** (with light-table registration ticks penciled on the margins by Graham!), then it makes 100% logical sense why he sliced three pages out of her sketchbook AND why he kept them in the packet! (Because you don't slice three pages out of your wife's expensive sketchbook just to use as blank scratch paper when you have reams of printer paper—you slice pages out of her sketchbook because they have her *real initials/signatures* on them to trace over on a light table!).
  - *Note:* In Ch. 2, line 79, Tessa saw `"three blank leaves show faint pressure lines where someone sliced pages straight out along the binding"`—the leaves *remaining* behind them were blank and showed pressure lines! Simply clarifying in Ch. 13/14 that the three sliced pages carried Lydia's authentic signed studies plus Graham's registration ticks/practice runs would make slicing her sketchbook infinitely smarter.
