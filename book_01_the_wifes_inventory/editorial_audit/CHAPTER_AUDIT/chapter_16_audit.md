## 4. Logic and Plausibility
- **[MEDIUM] Why Did Tessa Go Down Into a Dead-Zone Boathouse When Her Primary Goal Was Getting Cell Signal to Upload the Audio Packet? (Ch. 16, lines 29–33):**
  - *Passage (Ch. 16, lines 29–33):* `"The boathouse," I call back as Graham catches up to the archway. "Nothing's uploading from down here. Whatever they used the night of the storm is still sitting inside. Give me three minutes to shoot it, climb to the spruce ridge where signal holds, and push the file before Cole locks the point down."`
  - *Plausibility Analysis:*
    1. Tessa already has on her phone and thumb drive: (a) the unedited 5-channel WAV recording of Lydia Kiefer and Cole Halstead laughing and staging the drowning at 2:14 AM (`MIC_04_PANTRY_020915.wav`), (b) photos of the factory-clean life vest tags, and (c) the *Bramwell & Shaw* default notice and Cayman rollover rider—AND Cole Halstead's siren is **two minutes away**!
    2. Running *down* the cliff stairs into the cove (where cell reception is even worse under the granite bluff!) to break into the boathouse and take a photo of oars before climbing *up* to the spruce ridge to get LTE signal—when Graham literally just warned her in line 31 `"If Lydia's iPad just synced on local mesh, whoever is using it is still on this property"` and Tessa herself saw a flashlight inside the boathouse the evening before (Ch. 9, line 95)—is a high-risk decision driven partly by plot necessity (getting Tessa into the boathouse trap with Lydia).
    3. However, if climbing to the high spruce ridge from the back of the house without passing Cole's cruiser in the front turnaround requires taking the cove/cliff switchback trail (or if Tessa believes Lydia's iPad is broadcasting from the boathouse and wants to catch/photograph the staging source before escaping up the perimeter trail), that makes her route down the back deck stairs much more logical!
  - *Recommendation:* Flag as **[MEDIUM]** in `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`: in future revision, adding half a sentence in line 29 noting that Cole's cruiser is cutting off the front driveway/turnaround so the back cliff steps are the only unseen route around to the high spruce ridge makes Tessa's descent down the cliff steps 100% tactically necessary rather than an optional detour for more photos.
