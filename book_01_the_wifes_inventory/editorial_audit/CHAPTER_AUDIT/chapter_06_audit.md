  7. Inspecting the life vest in the mudroom, Tessa finds it factory-stiff with bright stainless buckles, zero sun fade or salt crust, and smelling of tap water and damp basement concrete rather than brine or kelp. She photographs the Coast Guard approval tag, serial number, and lot stamp inside the collar (2 shots, flash off).
  8. Tessa confronts Graham with Maren's question: whether the dock lights were on the night of October 14. Graham claims they shut the dock floodlights off at the sub-panel past mid-October because southeast salt mist trips the ground-fault loop and Lydia hated the glare.
  9. A heavy gust slams the house and knocks out grid power. While the diesel generator kicks in after a few seconds, Graham hurries to the smart-home wall tablet beside the gray steel breaker box in the pantry corridor (above which hangs a yellow service tag: `EXT-DECK / LOGS — 40M DIAGNOSTIC BYPASS BUS`).
  10. Half a second before Graham swipes the screen clear, Tessa sees a red-trimmed amber alert banner: **`MOTION DETECTED — BASEMENT ZONE 4 (PERIMETER BYPASS ACTIVE)`**. Graham claims power surges trip the old basement sensors, though his neck turns dark red. Across the hall, the flush wood door to Basement Zone 4 sits closed and untouched.
