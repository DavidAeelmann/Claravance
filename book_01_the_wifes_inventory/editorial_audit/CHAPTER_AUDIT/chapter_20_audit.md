## 4. Logic and Plausibility
- **[CONSISTENT] Handcuff Picking Mechanics (Ch. 20, lines 9, 17–21):**

In real-world lockpicking, single-locked standard Peerless/Smith & Wesson handcuffs can indeed be shimmed or levered open in seconds by pushing the spring-loaded ratchet pawl back through the keyway with a narrow awl or flat pick.
