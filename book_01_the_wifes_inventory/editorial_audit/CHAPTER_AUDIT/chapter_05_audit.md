  - **General Store Clerk** (skinny kid in a faded Patriots hoodie)
  - *Mentioned:* Samira Patel (marine-claims investigator in Portland), Tessa's landlord, Maren's brother-in-law.
- **Main Events:**
  1. Tessa drives across the causeway and takes the main harbor route past the Salt Point firehouse, where her phone signal jumps from one bar to four.
  2. She receives two missed calls from her landlord and an automated reminder from the Cumberland County clerk, then searches `Kiefer Soundworks Arlington`, finding a spring defense-tech press release about a pending patent buyout and a regional item noting Kiefer Soundworks missed its Q3 lab lease extension in Cambridge.
  3. Knox County Sheriff's Deputy **Cole Halstead** pulls her over on a pretext (right brake lamp out, low muffler shield). He expresses hostility toward mainland contractors poking through a local girl's things, warns her that the cove steps are slick and `"the boathouse is locked under county seal"`, and writes a yellow warning slip.
  4. Watching Halstead write, Tessa observes that he is **left-handed with a hooked wrist drag** (`"curls his left wrist high over the top of the paper, and drags the pen backward against the grain in a hard, hooked push"`), matching the smudged departure checklist in Lydia's studio. Halstead then pulls out heading straight toward the Salt Point causeway and Kiefer's house.
  5. In Salt Point harbor, Tessa pulls Lydia Kiefer's passport number from the public Coast Guard missing-person bulletin and sends an encrypted text to Portland marine-claims investigator **Samira Patel** (who has read-only access to the Atlantic Marine Underwriters' cross-border port-clearance watch) to flag any border hits.
  6. Inside the general store, Tessa buys two rolls of packing tape, permanent markers, and brake bulbs ($18.42 total).
  7. **Maren Lindqvist** enters, warns aloud that Friday's spring tide will top the causeway culvert if the wind swings east, and speaks quietly to Tessa by the coffee urns. Maren refuses to talk on the record because her aquaculture lease comes up for county sign-off in January and Cole Halstead's marine unit harasses her brother-in-law's boat, warning Tessa: `"Don't take Kiefer's word on when the gale hit, and don't turn your back on Cole Halstead when the tide comes up."`
  8. Passing Tessa's open brown paper store bag, Maren secretly drops a torn strip of glossy October tide-chart paper inside. Back in her Civic, Tessa finds it: **`Oct 15`** circled, **`2:14 AM — High Water`** boxed in carpenter's pencil, and block letters underneath reading: **`Ask him why his dock lights were off.`** Tessa folds it into her jeans watch pocket.
- **Purpose Within the Overall Story:** Introduces antagonist Deputy Cole Halstead in person and connects his left-handed hooked writing to the studio checklist; initiates the Samira Patel passport-watch query (which pays off at the end of Ch. 10); deepens Maren's alliance with Tessa; and delivers the critical `Oct 15 / 2:14 AM — High Water` tide-chart note that contradicts Graham's 10:30 PM–midnight timeline.

## 3. Continuity Verification
- **[VERIFY] Public Coast Guard Missing-Person Bulletin Including Full Passport Number (Ch. 5, line 59):**
  - *Passage (Ch. 5, line 59):* `"Before cutting the engine, I pull up the public Coast Guard missing-person bulletin for Lydia Kiefer, copy her passport number off the notice, and send an encrypted text to Samira Patel..."`
  - *Explanation:* In real-world U.S. Coast Guard public missing-person/mariner bulletins (and law enforcement BOLO flyers distributed to the public), a citizen's full 9-digit U.S. passport number (`#••••••3907` in Ch. 10, line 113) is PII and is never published on a *public* notice (it is shared on law enforcement/customs NCIC/TECS bulletins, or Samira Patel could pull it from the marine-underwriters' hull/owner policy dossier using Lydia's full name and DOB).
  - *Classification:* **[MEDIUM]** procedural realism issue. Easily fixed in revision by having Tessa copy Lydia's DOB/hull registration from the notice (or pull Lydia's passport number from the travel/estate folder in the house or let Samira query by Name + DOB).
- **[CONSISTENT] Left-Handed Hooked Writing Match (Ch. 5, line 43 vs. Ch. 2, lines 83–85 & Ch. 22–23):**
  - Cole's left-handed hooked writing posture in Ch. 5 directly echoes the curved left-margin wrist smear on the studio checklist in Ch. 2 and is forensically confirmed in Ch. 23.
- **[CONSISTENT] Watch Pocket & Evidence Separation (Ch. 5, line 109):**
  - Tessa places the folded tide-chart strip into her jeans watch pocket, separate from the tissue-wrapped `B-4` key in her jacket breast pocket, and keeps the yellow warning slip inside the brown store bag (hidden behind the guest-room radiator in Ch. 6, retrieved in Ch. 20, handed to Detective Ward in Ch. 22).
