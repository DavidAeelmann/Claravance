## 3. Continuity Verification
- **[HIGH] Front Door Deadbolt Mechanism Contradiction (Ch. 19, line 39 vs. Ch. 22, line 21):**
  - *Passage (Ch. 19, line 39):* `"Heavy boots hit the front slate overhead, followed by the rattle of the front deadbolt throwing shut from inside. Cole is back from the causeway."`
  - *Comparison (Ch. 22, lines 19–21):* `"I dig the brass front-door key out of my jeans with my free hand and toss it across the vestibule to Graham. 'Unlock the deadbolt. Now.' Graham catches it against his chest, stares at me for half a beat, then turns and works the double-keyed lock beside the sidelight."`
  - *Explanation:* In Ch. 19, line 39, Cole walks in the front door from outside (having unlocked or opened it) and throws the front deadbolt shut from the inside (`"the rattle of the front deadbolt throwing shut from inside"`—standard interior thumb-turn behavior, since Cole does not have Graham's key ring yet; three lines later in line 51 Lydia tells Cole: `"Grab Graham's key ring off the study desk"`!). Yet in Ch. 22, line 21, the front door deadbolt is abruptly described as `"the double-keyed lock beside the sidelight"` requiring Tessa to toss her brass front-door key to Graham so he can unlock it from the inside for the State Police!
  - *How to Reconcile:* If Cole had a copy of the front-door key on his sheriff's key ring (just as he had the boathouse padlock key in Ch. 17 line 91 and once had the `B-4` key before dropping it on Nov 1), OR if in Ch. 22 Graham simply turns the interior deadbolt latch (or Tessa orders Graham to open the deadbolt without needing to toss him a key for the interior side), the contradiction disappears completely.
- **[CONSISTENT] Left Wrist Injury Physical Constraint (Ch. 19, lines 35, 45, 57, 83):**
  - Tessa consistently protects and avoids loading her slashed left wrist on the iron ladder, hooking her elbows and bracing her back and knees against the hemlock studs instead of gripping with her injured left hand.
- **[CONSISTENT] Dumbwaiter Counterweight Mechanics (Ch. 11, lines 49, 59 -> Ch. 19, lines 59–75):**
  - Because the pig-iron counterweight runs inside `"the recessed side channel"` beside the iron ladder (line 73, matching Ch. 11, line 49: `"Down inside a wooden guide channel beside the ladder hangs the dumbwaiter's cast-iron counterweight"`), Tessa can pull the overhead pulley locking pin while hanging on the top three ladder rungs without the falling counterweight hitting her! The spatial engineering of the shaft was thoughtfully set up in Ch. 11.
