## 3. Continuity Verification
- **[CONSISTENT] Elapsed-Hours Arithmetic (`"Forty hours ago"` from Nov 3, 09:42 AST to Nov 5, 01:52 AM EST):**
  - *Passage (Ch. 11, line 5):* `"At 1:52 AM... Lydia Kiefer's St. Andrews timestamp sits burned behind my eyelids: November 3, 09:42 AST. Forty hours ago, her passport was scanned at a private dock in New Brunswick..."`
  - *Verification:* `Nov 3, 09:42 AST` is `Nov 3, 08:42 EST` (since Atlantic Standard Time is UTC-4 and Eastern Standard Time is UTC-5; Daylight Saving Time in 2026 ends on Sunday, November 1, 2026!). From `Nov 3, 08:42 EST` to `Nov 5, 01:52 AM EST` is **41 hours and 10 minutes** (or from 09:42 to 01:52 is 40 hours and 10 minutes). Calling that `"Forty hours ago"` in internal monologue is remarkably accurate!
- **[HIGH] Timing Anomaly: What Was the Intruder Doing in the Dumbwaiter Shaft at 2:27 AM vs. Why Did They Lock Tessa in at 2:30 AM? (Ch. 11, lines 11–77 vs. Ch. 12, line 67 & Ch. 14, line 33):**
  - *Trace the exact sequence between 2:27 AM and 2:34 AM:*
    1. At **2:27 AM** (Ch. 11, line 11), the intruder (Lydia/Cole) climbs *out* of the dumbwaiter shaft into the pantry and latches the two recessed brass flush-latches from inside the pantry (`"It sounds like a heavy latch dropping home inside a wall near the pantry, followed by a low hum vibrating through the baseboard pipes"`), AND puts the stoneware crocks and spice jars back on the middle shelf! (Wait: in line 31, Tessa has to clear `"the heavy stoneware crocks, paprika tins, and glass spice jars down onto a folded dish towel... With the middle shelf cleared, I run my fingers along the seam... Two recessed brass flush-latches sit countersunk into the dark grain, hidden behind the jars"`!)
    2. Less than 3–5 minutes later (~2:30–2:32 AM), Tessa walks down the hall, takes the `B-4` key from her jacket in the mudroom, enters the pantry, clears the jars, opens the flush-latches, climbs down the ladder, trips on the mic cord, and wakes the dual monitors.
    3. A couple minutes later (Ch. 12, line 67; clock reads **2:34 AM** in Ch. 13, line 21), the intruder is *still inside the house*, cracks the Basement Zone 4 steel fire door from the stairwell, sees Tessa / the lit monitors, throws the exterior deadbolt on the fire door, walks into the kitchen, jams a wooden kitchen chair under the walk-in pantry doorknob, turns a deadbolt by the mudroom, and walks out!
  - *Two Plausibility/Continuity Questions Here:*
    - **Question A (The Spice Jars on the Hinged Panel):** In Ch. 11, line 43, Tessa notes: `"The whole back wall—shelf cleats and all—swings out on hidden piano hinges."` Wait: if the *entire back wall, shelf cleats and all*, swings outward into the pantry on piano hinges when the two flush-latches are turned, how did someone climbing *out* of the shaft into the pantry at 2:27 AM latch the two countersunk flush-latches *behind the spice jars* and replace all the heavy stoneware crocks and spice jars in the pitch dark in seconds, OR if the shelves swing *with* the back wall, why couldn't the flush-latches be operated from inside the shaft unless the latches are only on the pantry side? More importantly, if the intruder just exited the pantry at 2:27 AM, where was the intruder hiding between 2:27 AM and 2:30 AM while Tessa walked down the north hall, into the mudroom, and into the pantry?!
    - **Let's check Graham's explanation in Ch. 14 (line 33) and Lydia's in Ch. 14 (line 113) / Ch. 15 (line 127):**
      - In Ch. 14, line 33, Graham says: `"Whoever dropped down that dumbwaiter shaft earlier couldn't crack my thirty-two-character passphrase or force the archive cabinet. They climbed back out into the kitchen, lifted my keys while I was out, cracked the basement door when they saw the displays wake up—saw you down there—and slammed the deadbolt home before wedging a chair against the pantry to seal the shaft and running out the front."`
      - And in Ch. 14, line 113, they also popped Tessa's Civic trunk (using her stolen spare key fob) and planted Lydia's muddy duck boots, the navy tote, the bleach rag, and the bronze cleat!
      - So at 2:27 AM, when the intruder climbed out of the dumbwaiter shaft and closed the pantry panel, the intruder went into the living room/guest room or stepped out the front door to Tessa's Civic in the turnaround to plant the boots/cleat in her trunk—and then stepped back inside the unlatched front door (or was in the study/living room lifting Graham's keys off the floor by the sofa) while Tessa was in the pantry/shaft, heard or saw the basement displays/status wake up at 2:33 AM, and locked Tessa in!
    - *Assessment:* That timing actually works if the intruder stepped out to the turnaround (or was in the study/living room shadow) right at 2:28 AM while Tessa was inside the closed walk-in pantry clearing the shelf! However, having the intruder carefully restack heavy stoneware crocks and paprika tins in front of the countersunk flush-latches at 2:27 AM—only for Tessa to unstack them two minutes later—is slightly clunky if the shelves are attached to the swinging door anyway (`"The whole back wall—shelf cleats and all—swings out on hidden piano hinges"`). If the jars are only blocking access to the two recessed latch slots, moving two or three jars aside to reach the slots makes total sense.
