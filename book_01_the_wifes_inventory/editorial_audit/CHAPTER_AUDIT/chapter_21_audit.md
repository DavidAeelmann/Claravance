  14. Clutching her bleeding right sleeve and the small portable backup drive from the study, Lydia scrambles up the basement stairs ahead of Tessa, sprints into the front vestibule, drags the cedar coat bench under the low skylight hatch, cranks the brass latch open, and grabs for the wet roof framing in the pouring rain—just as **red and blue police light bars and twin sirens** flare through the fog above the spruces.
