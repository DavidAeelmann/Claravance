## 4. Logic and Plausibility
- **[MEDIUM] Why Would Someone Flush Crushed Zolpidem Down the Master Bath Sink P-Trap?**
  - *Passage (Ch. 7, lines 63–69 & Ch. 17, line 63):* Tessa finds a chalky white residue ring around the left sink stopper and damp white crystalline paste caked inside the P-trap bend. In Ch. 10 (line 17) and Ch. 14 (line 29), someone had "dusted" Graham's rocks glass on the living-room credenza with zolpidem while he was in Portland, and in Ch. 17 (line 63), Tessa says to Lydia: `"I bagged a swab of the paste you flushed down the master bath P-trap before dusting Graham's rocks glass."`
  - *Plausibility Question:*
    1. When did Lydia crush the zolpidem in the master bathroom and rinse the excess down the left sink? Tessa finds the paste in the P-trap at **~10:45 AM on Wednesday morning** *before* Cole Halstead enters the house at 11:00 AM! That means Lydia must have crushed the pills in the master bathroom on **Tuesday night / early Wednesday morning** (or earlier) before Graham left at 7:15 AM and before Tessa went up to the master suite around 7:30–8:00 AM Wednesday!
    2. Furthermore, zolpidem tartrate 10 mg tablets are tiny (~150–200 mg total tablet weight including excipients; 10 tablets = ~1.5–2 grams total powder). Even if someone crushed 10 tablets on the vanity and rinsed the mortar/crusher or discarded half in the sink without running enough water to clear the P-trap, yielding `"a damp smear of white crystalline paste caked inside the lower curve of the pipe"` requires that very little water was run afterward (which is supported by line 45: `"a faint water ring near the left brass faucet"`!).
    3. However, why would Lydia (or Cole) crush pills in the *upstairs master bathroom* and rinse residue into the sink before dusting Graham's rocks glass in the *downstairs living room*? Because the Ambien prescription vial was kept in the master bathroom medicine cabinet! And putting the vial with the remaining 20 pills back behind the ibuprofen bottle so the bottle wouldn't look missing is actually quite clever.
