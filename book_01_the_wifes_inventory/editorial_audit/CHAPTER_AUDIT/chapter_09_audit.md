Per Part 3.7 instructions (`"Do not silently substitute real-world assumptions for the fictional rules established by the manuscript"`), we document both the internal consistency of the manuscript's rule and the real-world maritime distinction in `LEGAL_AND_TECHNICAL_BIBLE.md`.
