## 4. Logic and Plausibility
- **[MEDIUM] Decision to Spend 7 Hours (8:00 AM – 3:30 PM) Photographing Upstairs Closets Before Decrypting the Thumb Drive or Closing the Civic Trunk (Ch. 14, lines 109–117 & Ch. 15, lines 3–7):**
  - *Passage (Ch. 14, lines 115–117 & Ch. 15, lines 3–7):* At 6:30 AM, after learning that Lydia is alive, Cole Halstead has co-owner installer PINs, someone locked Tessa in the basement at 2:34 AM, and someone just popped Tessa's Civic trunk and planted a bleach-stained rag, a pried boathouse cleat, and Lydia's muddy duck boots to frame both of them for murder, Tessa demands the 32-character passphrase for the thumb drive. Graham says: `"Not until you finish the upstairs inventory photos for the Arlington probate packet... Shoot the rooms; I'll mount the drive this afternoon."` And then in Ch. 15 (lines 3–7), Tessa spends **seven hours** (until 3:30 PM!) photographing upstairs shelves and linen closets while leaving her Civic trunk standing popped open in the rain and the muddy duck boots sitting on the foyer floor!
  - *Plausibility Issue:*
    1. Entering a 32-character passphrase to mount a thumb drive takes **30 seconds** (as shown in Ch. 15, lines 30–63, where mounting and opening the directory takes under two minutes!). Even if Graham insisted Tessa finish the upstairs inventory photos so he could upload the schedule by 5:00 PM, why would Tessa agree to wait **seven hours** until 3:30 PM before taking two minutes to listen to the thumb drive—and why would they leave the muddy duck boots dripping on the foyer slate and her Civic trunk popped open in the turnaround for nine hours from 6:05 AM to 3:30 PM (`"'We can't leave those boots sitting on the floor,' Graham says behind me, his voice flat and hoarse after seven hours of quiet while I photographed every shelf and closet upstairs"`)?!
    2. While dramatically convenient for placing the study decryption scene at **3:30 PM** (right before the afternoon gale peaks and the 5:15 PM high-tide surge floods the causeway!), waiting seven hours to clean up the puddle on the foyer slate and mount a thumb drive stretches real-world urgency.
  - *Recommended Action:* Flag as **[HIGH]** in `FINAL_AUDIT_REPORT.md` and `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`. In future revision, this 7-hour gap can be made much more plausible with a single sentence in Ch. 14/15: e.g., Graham's study workstation / RAID decryption utility requires a multi-hour parity rebuild or integrity check after the 4:45 PM triple-lockout before it will accept the master passphrase (or Graham refuses to type the key until the inventory spreadsheet hits 100% upload at 3:30 PM, and Tessa shuts her trunk lid or leaves it untouched to avoid putting her prints on the latch).
