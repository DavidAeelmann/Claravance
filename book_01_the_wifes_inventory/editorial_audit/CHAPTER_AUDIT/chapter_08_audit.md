## 3. Continuity Verification
- **[MEDIUM] Timeline / Causality Question on Cole's Burner Call ("Move it up to Thursday" vs. "Don't give a damn what the Boston courier thinks"):**
  - *Passage (Ch. 8, lines 87–93):*
    - Line 87: `"Portland cleaner's dragging her feet in the master closet," he mutters. "Move it up to Thursday."`
    - Line 89: `*Move what up?* Graham already shoved the probate filing up to Thursday night over soup yesterday. Whoever is on the other end of that burner isn't Graham Kiefer—they're accelerating a separate move of their own to beat the Boston courier.`
    - Line 93: `"Don't give a damn what the Boston courier thinks," Halstead says...`
  - *Analysis:*
    1. Notice that in Ch. 6 (line 17), on **Tuesday evening**, Graham told Tessa: `"Boston counsel called while you were out. They moved the preliminary filing window up to Thursday night."` And in Ch. 7 (line 11), on **Wednesday morning at 7:15 AM**, Graham told Tessa: `"They're sending a courier tomorrow evening [Thursday] once I upload the preliminary ledger at five."`
    2. So why is Cole saying on Wednesday at 11:00 AM, `"Portland cleaner's dragging her feet in the master closet. Move it up to Thursday... Don't give a damn what the Boston courier thinks"`? Because Lydia and Cole originally planned their final move (staging Graham's suicide/murder-suicide) for **Friday** (matching the original Friday probate deadline), and now—either because Boston moved the courier to Thursday night or because Tessa is poking around too much—Cole and Lydia are moving *their* strike up to **Thursday** (before the Thursday 5:00 PM courier arrives, whom Lydia later cancels in Ch. 17, line 55!).
    3. That internal logic holds together well, as Tessa explicitly notes in line 89 (`"they're accelerating a separate move of their own to beat the Boston courier"`).
- **[MEDIUM] Question on What Cole Actually Accomplished During His Wednesday 11:00 AM Visit (Ch. 8, lines 53–57, 93 vs. Ch. 10, line 17 & Ch. 15, line 21):**
  - *Observation:* Tessa watches Cole from the moment he opens the breaker box (Ch. 8, line 3) until he unclips the jumper wire and exits out the glass deck door (line 101). During that entire time on screen, Cole only: (a) bridges the breaker panel, (b) checks the wall tablet and wipes his entry log, (c) goes into the mudroom to check the life vest, the mudroom side door deadbolt, and the boot tray, (d) wipes his muddy heel print off the kitchen slate with a wet paper towel, (e) takes the burner call, and (f) unbridges the panel and leaves!
  - *Wait:* When did someone **dust Graham's dry bourbon tumbler on the living-room credenza** (Ch. 10, line 17; Ch. 14, line 29) and **steal Tessa's spare Civic key fob from her duffel in the guest room** (Ch. 15, line 21)?
    - Could Cole have done it during this visit? No—Tessa watched him the entire time he was inside from the moment he stepped through the glass deck door and opened the breaker panel five feet away!
    - Could **Lydia** have entered the house separately on Wednesday (e.g., while Tessa went down to Maren's sorting shed from 4:30 PM to ~5:15 PM in Ch. 9!)?
    - **Let's check Chapter 12, line 19–21!** In Ch. 12 (lines 19–21), Tessa discovers on the basement computer: `"Right underneath sits a red terminal alert stamped 4:45 PM: three failed passphrase lockouts on the encrypted export queue. At four-forty-five yesterday, Graham's Audi was ninety miles south on I-95, and I was down at Maren's sorting shed. Somebody stood right at this table trying to break into the export queue while the house sat empty—just minutes before I spotted a flashlight moving behind the boathouse window down on the cove."`
    - **BINGO!** At **4:45 PM Wednesday**, while Tessa was down at Maren's sorting shed (4:30–5:15 PM), Lydia (or Cole/Lydia via the dumbwaiter shaft) was *inside the empty house*! That 4:30–5:15 PM window when Tessa left the house unattended is when Lydia/Cole accessed the living room credenza (dusting Graham's dry rocks glass with zolpidem), stole Tessa's spare Civic key fob from her guest-room duffel, and climbed down the dumbwaiter shaft to try cracking Graham's basement server at 4:45 PM before retreating to the boathouse!
  - *Still, why did Cole come inside at 11:00 AM in Ch. 8 and leave after 5 minutes without taking anything?* In line 43, Tessa deduces: `"Now he knows I didn't drive back into town; as far as he can tell, I'm still upstairs in the master suite... which means he's gambling I stay put long enough for him to finish."` And when his burner rings and he learns/decides to wait or sees Tessa is home, he aborts (`"I'll pull the rest before any suit from Boston sets foot on the causeway"`). This is logically coherent!
