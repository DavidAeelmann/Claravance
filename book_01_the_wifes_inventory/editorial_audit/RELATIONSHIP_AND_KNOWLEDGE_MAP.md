## 3. Knowledge & POV Audit Findings

1. **[CONSISTENT] Protagonist Knowledge Discipline (Chapters 1–23):**
   - Across the entire manuscript, Tessa Calder **never** uses a character's name, a PIN code, a security feature, a financial figure, or a back-story detail before the narrative explicitly shows how she learned it:
     - She learns *Bramwell & Shaw*, *5.4 million*, *Deputy Halstead*, and *Maren Lindqvist* from Graham in Chapter 3.
     - She learns Cole's first name (*Cole*) and his left-handedness during the traffic stop in Chapter 5.
     - She learns of the `EXT-DECK / LOGS — 40M DIAGNOSTIC BYPASS BUS` from the yellow conduit tag in Chapter 6 before seeing Cole bridge it with a jumper wire in Chapter 8.
     - She feels the hollow wall behind the bottom pantry spice shelf with her own spine in Chapter 8 before discovering the recessed flush-latches of the dumbwaiter shaft in Chapter 11.
     - She sees `BOATHOUSE_LINT` on the Zone 4 audio editor in Chapter 12 and in the raw transcript in Chapter 13 before spotting the live `BOATHOUSE_LINT` PoE microphone puck under the boathouse bait table in Chapter 17.
2. **[MEDIUM] Single First-Person POV Omniscience Slip (Chapter 20, Line 89):**
   - *Passage:* `"Before we clear three paces, a heavy mechanical CLUNK carries up through the floorboards from the basement utility alcove—Lydia throwing the generator transfer switch off and pulling the manual fail-safe lever beside the panel."`
   - *Issue:* Standing in the ground-floor north hall, Tessa cannot see through the floorboards into a "basement utility alcove" she has never visited or observe Lydia pulling a manual fail-safe lever beside the panel.
   - *Fix:* Have Tessa hear the mechanical *CLUNK* from the basement and see the power die and shutters drop in Ch. 20, lines 89–93, and let Graham state in Ch. 21, line 9 that Lydia threw the generator transfer switch and manual shutter release in the basement utility alcove.
