## 1. Quick-Reference Card for Future Editorial Agents

- **Title:** *The Wife's Inventory* (Book 1 of the Tessa Calder / Claravance Trilogy)
- **Word Count & Structure:** 23 Chapters (~40,164 words total; Chapters 1–22 span 4 consecutive days from Monday, Nov 2 to Thursday, Nov 5, 2026; Chapter 23 is an epilogue four weeks later on Monday, Dec 7, 2026).
- **POV & Tense:** First-person singular (`"I"` — **Tessa Calder**), **present tense** throughout.
- **Genre & Tone:** Commercial domestic/coastal psychological thriller & forensic investigative mystery; atmospheric, hard-boiled, blue-collar procedural grounding with gothic-modern architectural suspense.
- **Protagonist:** **Tessa Calder**, a blacklisted former commercial fraud claims investigator at **Harbor Mutual** in Portland, Maine (fired in 2023 for refusing to sign a fraudulent Commercial Street fire manifest without chain-of-custody; owes **$18,412** remaining on a **$42,000** defamation judgment with **$400 and change** in her checking account and a bank levy scheduled for Monday, Nov 9).
- **The Assignment:** **$15,000 cash** ($5,000 upfront in a Portsmouth bank band; $10,000 completion, later supplemented by a **$3,500** storm adjustment to **$13,500** via *Kiefer-Hall Fiduciary* escrow) under a **$50,000 liquidated-damages NDA** (amended in the margin by `G.K.` to allow timestamped estate-verification photos) to inventory the personal property of **Lydia Kiefer**, who purportedly drowned on **Wednesday, October 14, 2026** in a skiff off **Salt Point, Maine** (Knox County, Penobscot Bay).
- **The Dual Central Truth:**
  1. **Graham Kiefer's Crime (Financial Forgery):** Over 14 months, Graham pulled **$2,400,000** in six unauthorized draws from *The Lydia Kiefer Irrevocable Trust* ($5.4M corpus, leaving $3.0M) to keep his defense acoustics company (**Kiefer Soundworks LLC**) afloat while an Arlington defense buyout sat in federal export review. On **October 10, 2026**, Graham forged a retroactive trust ratification packet by slicing three pages out of Lydia's studio sketchbook to trace her `L.K.` initials on a light table. On **October 15**, Boston trustees (**Bramwell & Shaw Fiduciary LLP**) issued a *Notice of Default & Acceleration* with a **30-day civil cure window expiring November 14, 2026**.
  2. **Lydia Kiefer & Deputy Cole Halstead's Crime (Staged Drowning & Double Murder Frame):** Rather than divorcing Graham or waiting for civil recovery, **Lydia Kiefer** conspired with her childhood friend **Knox County Sheriff's Deputy Cole Halstead** to fake her drowning at **02:14 AM on October 15, 2026** and frame Graham for murder. Under the trust's governance rider and **Cayman beneficiary rollover schedule**, a formal murder charge (or Graham's death with a signed confession) immediately strips Graham's signatory rights and cure window and transfers the remaining **$3,000,000** out of Boston probate into an offshore Cayman holding trust established by Lydia's father (for which Lydia scanned her US Passport `#••••••3907` in person at **St. Andrews Private Marina, New Brunswick** on **Tuesday, Nov 3, 2026 at 09:42 AST**). Once Graham hired Tessa as an investigative tripwire (`CALDER_T — VETTING`, Oct 21), Lydia and Cole pivoted to framing Tessa as Graham's paid accomplice and staging a double murder-suicide during Thursday's 5:15 PM high-tide storm surge.

8. **Margot Kiefer-Hall:** Managing partner at **Kiefer-Hall Fiduciary** (State Street, Boston); distant cousin of Graham who holds the independent archiving escrow, adds the $3,500 storm bonus to Tessa's $10,000 completion check ($13,500 total), and meets Tessa at Becky's Diner on Monday, Dec 7, 2026 to recruit her for a $45,000 10-day winter inventory of a snowbound mountain lodge in northern Vermont—presenting a photo backed by a faxed guest ledger entry from Dec 5, 2026 bearing **Nina Calder's** handwriting (`Don't trust him.`, Ch. 23).
