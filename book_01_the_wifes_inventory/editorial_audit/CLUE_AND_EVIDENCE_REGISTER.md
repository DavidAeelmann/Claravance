## 2. Evidence-Handling & Forensic Integrity Summary

1. **Zero Forgotten or Vanishing Physical Items:**
   - Every item Tessa pockets, hides, drops, or photographs across all 23 chapters is explicitly tracked to its final disposition in Chapters 22–23.
   - Particularly impressive is the tracking of Tessa's pockets before, during, and after her plunge into Salt Point Cove in Chapter 18: her smartphone is kicked under the lobster traps and crushed into the slipway before she jumps (and fished off a submerged cross-beam in Ch. 22); Maren's tide strip is soaked in her watch pocket and left behind on Maren's shed latch; and the encrypted thumb drive, `B-4` key, brass front-door key, multi-tool, and knotted nitrile glove swab remain in her pockets and are transferred into her duffel in Ch. 20 and handed to Detective Sergeant Ward in Ch. 22.
2. **Strict Evidence-vs.-Inference Discipline Maintained by Protagonist:**
   - Tessa consistently notes when a sensory detail or clue is insufficient on its own without corroboration:
     - *Ch. 2 (line 65, Painted-shut window):* `"Maybe maintenance painted it shut months ago. Or maybe somebody sealed it after Lydia vanished..."`
     - *Ch. 4 (line 29, Linseed oil smell):* `"The smell alone doesn't date anything—heavy wool traps fumes. But the oil still has tack under my fingertips, and the molding above it has been disturbed."`
     - *Ch. 6 (line 75, Tap-water life vest):* `"Somebody could have rinsed a vest in a laundry sink or hung up a spare from a boat locker—my nose alone won't win an argument."`
     - *Ch. 7 (line 61 & 69, Missing Ambien & P-trap paste):* `"Ten missing over ten days could mean one a night before she vanished—nothing strange on its own... Without a lab, it could be scouring powder or mineral buildup—except nobody scrubs a spotless vanity drain and leaves wet paste packed in the bend."`
