# 01 — Anti-AI Prose Style Guide (*The Wife's Inventory*)

## 1. Point of View & Tense Contract
- **Chapters 1–16 & 19–25 (Clara Vance):** First-person present tense (`I`). Immediate, forensic, hyper-observant, dry under pressure.
- **Chapters 17–18 (Evelyn Mercer Flashbacks):** Third-person limited past tense (`she`), cold and calculating, sharply differentiated from Clara’s tactile present voice.

## 2. Hard-Banned Lexicon (Zero-Tolerance Audit List)
Any occurrence in a draft triggers automatic Critic rejection and rewrite:
- **Abstract LLM Nouns/Metaphors:** `testament`, `tapestry`, `mosaic`, `symphony`, `cacophony`, `kaleidoscope`, `beacon`, `labyrinth`, `abyss`, `chasm`, `maelstrom`, `facade`, `orchestration`, `enigma`, `paradox`.
- **Sensory & Thriller Clichés:** `ozone`, `palpable`, `shiver down`, `chill down`, `ice in [my/her] veins`, `blood ran cold`, `breath hitched`, `let out a breath`, `knuckles turned white`, `silence stretched`, `eyes narrowed`, `throat tightened`, `stomach dropped`, `wave of dread`, `wave of relief`, `wave of panic`, `flicker of`, `chuckle`, `smirk`, `steely gaze`.
- **Syntactic Crutches:** `"It wasn't [X]—it was [Y]"` / `"It wasn't [X]; it was [Y]"`, `"Little did [I/she] know"`, `"In that moment"`, `"Nothing would ever be the same"`, `"Before I knew it"`, `"A reminder that"`.

## 3. Structural & Craft Rules
1. **Asymmetric Sentence Cadence:** Never write three medium sentences (12–16 words) consecutively. Alternate short, blunt fragments (2–6 words) with crisp observational clauses (18–26 words).
2. **Participial Opener Cap:** Maximum 1 sentence starting with an `-ing` verb phrase per 500 words (ban *"Walking into the kitchen, I saw..."* → *"I pushed the kitchen door open. Cold draft hit my ankles."*).
3. **No Summary or Moralizing Scene Endings:** Never close a scene or chapter with Clara summarizing what she learned or how she feels. **Cut two beats early** on a concrete object, physical contradiction, or interrupted motion.
4. **Cornered Thriller Protagonist (Interior Stakes > Stoic Noir):** Trade hardboiled Jack Reacher stoicism for real psychological vulnerability, exhaustion, resentment, and claustrophobia. Avoid generic emotion-label clichés (*"a wave of dread"*), but put the reader inside Clara's financial desperation and trapped dread—what it costs her to stay in a glass house with a potential killer when walking out means losing everything.
5. **The "No Tape Measure" Rule (Cap Numerical Measurements):** Eliminate surveyor geometry (inches, feet, yards, degrees, pounds, fractions, exact seconds). Replace measurements with visceral impression (*narrow, low, freezing, heavy*). Reserve exact numbers **strictly** for financial math (debt, cash, NDA penalties) and forensic timestamps/dates. Maximum 4 physical unit numbers per chapter.
6. **Cut Redundant "Negative Lists":** Never describe rooms by listing multiple absent items (*"No woodsmoke. No stale coffee. No wet umbrellas."*). Use the absent-object device at most **once per chapter**, only when the absence is a forensic clue.
7. **Purposeful Micro-Actions Only:** Do not narrate granular body/object mechanics (zipping to a corner, dodging doormat fibers) unless Clara is deliberately setting a tamper-trap—and if she is, give half a sentence of interior thought so the reader knows why.
8. **Plausible Clue Placement:** Meticulous antagonists never leave smoking guns in plain sight by accident. Either bury clues inside logical blind spots (closets, linings, baseboards) or explicitly register Clara's paranoia that the clue was planted as bait.
9. **Subtext-First Dialogue:** Characters lie by deflecting to logistics, offering tea/coats, adjusting objects, or answering adjacent questions. Use `said` or physical action beats for 90%+ of attributions; ban adverbs on dialogue tags (*"he said coldly"*).
10. **Non-Visual Sensory Grounding:** Every scene must anchor at least two non-visual details (damp cedar, bleach residue, wool static, radiator tick, diesel exhaust, metallic sink crust).

## 4. Scene-Expansion Pacing Rule
- Each ~2,500-word chapter MUST be drafted across **3 discrete ~800-word scene beats** (Entry/Friction → Escalation/Subtext → Physical Discovery/Early Cut). Never compress or summarize transitions.

## 5. Calibration Reference (Do vs. Don't)
- **REJECTED:** *Walking into the study, a palpable sense of dread washed over me, sending a shiver down my spine. The room was a testament to Julian's wealth, filled with a tapestry of secrets. Silence stretched between us as my breath hitched.*
- **APPROVED:** *The study smelled like wet cedar and lemon oil. No dust on the baseboards. Someone had scrubbed this room within the last forty-eight hours, though Julian claimed he hadn't crossed the threshold since the Coast Guard called off the search. On the desk sat a brass letter opener—blade wiped dry, handle still tacky near the hilt.*
