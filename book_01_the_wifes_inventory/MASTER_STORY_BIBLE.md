## 3. Chronological Sequence of Lydia's Conspiracy

### Phase 1: Original Escape Plan (Oct 10 – Oct 17)
1. **Trigger:** Lydia discovers Graham forged her initials to drain $2.4M from the $5.4M family trust. Reporting civil embezzlement would preserve Graham's 30-day cure window (to Nov 14) and lock the remaining $3.0M in protracted *Kiefer Soundworks* bankruptcy/divorce litigation.

Lydia & Cole escalate from a clean vanishing act to a **murder frame** so a felony charge triggers the trust's moral-turpitude forfeiture clause immediately.

### Phase 3: Tactical Pivot Against Tessa & Graham (Nov 2 – Nov 5)
