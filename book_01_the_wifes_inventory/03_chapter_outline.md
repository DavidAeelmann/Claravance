# 03 — 25-Chapter Beat-by-Beat Blueprint (*The Wife's Inventory*)

**Target Word Count:** ~62,500 words (25 chapters × ~2,500 words; drafted as 3 discrete ~800-word scene beats per chapter).

---

## Act I: Arrival, House Rules & First Cracks (Chapters 1–6)

### Chapter 1
- **POV & Setting:** Clara (1st Present); Salt Point tidal causeway & Mercer Glass House foyer (Monday, Nov 4, dusk).
- **Micro-Goal & Conflict:** Clara needs her $5,000 upfront envelope and room key; Julian withholds payment until she signs the NDA and memorizes his three strict rules (log all Evelyn's belongings, stay out of the boathouse, never open the basement acoustic room).
- **Value Shift:** Outsider contractor seeking a routine paycheck → Trapped insider bound by a suspiciously rigid NDA.
- **Cliffhanger / Page-Turn Hook:** Opening the foyer coat closet to hang her wet jacket, Clara finds Evelyn’s packed olive-canvas weekender tucked behind Julian's winter coats—tagged with a ferry stub dated *October 17*, three days *after* Evelyn drowned, leaving Clara wondering whether Julian hid it poorly or planted it to test her.

### Chapter 2
- **POV & Setting:** Clara; Guest suite & Evelyn’s upstairs studio (Day 0, night).
- **Micro-Goal & Conflict:** Clara begins logging Evelyn’s studio desk to look compliant while testing if the room was staged; she finds the sash window freshly painted shut from inside despite Julian claiming Evelyn aired it daily.
- **Value Shift:** Mild curiosity → Active forensic suspicion of a staged disappearance.
- **Cliffhanger / Page-Turn Hook:** Clara inspects the silver Montblanc pen on the blotter—noting left-handed nib wear on a right-handed woman's desk—right as footsteps stop outside the closed door and the knob turns slowly.

### Chapter 3
- **POV & Setting:** Clara; Mercer kitchen & back deck (Day 1 morning, low tide).
- **Micro-Goal & Conflict:** Clara tries to baseline Julian's storm-night timeline over coffee; Julian deflects with grief-stricken charm while probing whether Clara knows about his stalled acoustic patent buyout.
- **Value Shift:** Sees Julian as coldly secretive → Sees Julian as financially cornered (clear motive).
- **Cliffhanger / Page-Turn Hook:** Down by the cove dock, neighbor Maren hauls traps and holds up two fingers in a silent warning before nodding toward the locked boathouse.

### Chapter 4
- **POV & Setting:** Clara; Sunroom & mudroom (Day 1 afternoon).
- **Micro-Goal & Conflict:** Cataloging rugs and linens, Clara shifts the heavy gray wool rug in the sunroom and smells fresh bleach and linseed oil over a scrubbed cedar patch.
- **Value Shift:** Speculative hunch → Physical evidence of post-disappearance cleanup.
- **Cliffhanger / Page-Turn Hook:** Probing the damp baseboard seam, a rusted brass key drops out—stamped with the basement door number.

### Chapter 5
- **POV & Setting:** Clara; Harbor road & Salt Point general store (Day 1 late afternoon).
- **Micro-Goal & Conflict:** Clara drives into town for packing boxes and local intel; Deputy Cole Halstead pulls her over for a dim taillight, warns her off "poking around Julian's grief," and signs the slip **left-handed**.
- **Value Shift:** Independent contractor → Monitored target under local police scrutiny.
- **Cliffhanger / Page-Turn Hook:** Back in her Civic, Clara finds a torn tide-chart slipped into her store receipt bag by Maren: *2:14 AM — High Water. Ask him why his dock lights were off.*

### Chapter 6
- **POV & Setting:** Clara; Dining room & mudroom (Day 2 evening, storm squall).
- **Micro-Goal & Conflict:** Clara baits Julian by asking for the serial number on the mudroom life jacket for probate records; Julian lies about Coast Guard return rules, unaware Clara sees the canvas straps are crisp and salt-free.
- **Value Shift:** Passive observer → Active investigator deliberately testing Julian's alibi *(Act I Break)*.
- **Cliffhanger / Page-Turn Hook:** Power flickers; as the backup generator engages, the wall tablet reboots and flashes a live motion ping in **Basement Zone 4** before wiping to "Offline."

## Act IIa: The Inventory Trap & Escalation (Chapters 7–13)

### Chapter 7
- **POV & Setting:** Clara; Master closet & bath (Day 3 morning, Julian in Portland).
- **Micro-Goal & Conflict:** Using Julian’s four-hour absence, Clara audits Evelyn’s meds and jewelry; she finds 28/30 Ambien pills intact and white crystalline powder dried in the sink's brass U-bend.
- **Value Shift:** Assumes Julian drugged Evelyn → Spots a structural contradiction (dissolved pills without ingestion).
- **Cliffhanger / Page-Turn Hook:** Clara's phone buzzes with a doorbell motion alert—Deputy Cole Halstead is standing on the back deck, punching a code into the pantry keypad.

### Chapter 8
- **POV & Setting:** Clara; Pantry corridor & service stairs (Day 3 midday).
- **Micro-Goal & Conflict:** Clara hides inside the cedar pantry as Cole enters with a valid PIN and disables exterior deck camera logs for 40 minutes at the breaker box.
- **Value Shift:** Thinks Cole is covering for Julian → Realizes Cole has autonomous private access to the house.
- **Cliffhanger / Page-Turn Hook:** Cole pauses inches from Clara's pantry door and mutters into his phone: *"She's taking too long in the master suite. Move the deadline to Thursday."*

### Chapter 9
- **POV & Setting:** Clara; Cove trail & Maren’s oyster shack (Day 3 dusk).
- **Micro-Goal & Conflict:** Clara confronts Maren on the rocky cove; Maren refuses names out of fear for her license, but admits Evelyn visited her shack two days *before* the storm asking how to kill a boat's GPS transponder.
- **Value Shift:** Evelyn as passive victim → Evelyn as active planner before the squall.
- **Cliffhanger / Page-Turn Hook:** Climbing back up the cliff path in darkness, Clara sees a flashlight sweep the *locked boathouse* windows—just as Julian’s Audi turns into the front drive.

### Chapter 10
- **POV & Setting:** Clara; Living room & study (Day 3 night).
- **Micro-Goal & Conflict:** Julian pours drinks and presses Clara to finish packing by Friday; Clara swaps her glass while he fetches ice and photographs an unshredded demand letter showing his firm owes $2.4M to Evelyn’s trust.
- **Value Shift:** Confirms massive financial motive against Julian.
- **Cliffhanger / Page-Turn Hook:** Clara's dormant insurance-database watch pings her phone: Evelyn’s passport cleared a private marina scanner in St. Andrews, New Brunswick, *yesterday*.

### Chapter 11
- **POV & Setting:** Clara; Pantry corridor & concealed utility shaft (Day 4, 2:00 AM).
- **Micro-Goal & Conflict:** Hearing a metallic latch click at 2:14 AM, Clara pries behind the pantry spice rack and uncovers the manual dumbwaiter shaft running down to the basement.
- **Value Shift:** Shut out of locked zones → Gains covert vertical access into the blind spot.
- **Cliffhanger / Page-Turn Hook:** Dropping off the iron ladder into the darkroom, Clara trips a studio monitor displaying a frozen frame of *Julian* dragging a rolled canvas rug toward the boathouse hatch.

### Chapter 12
- **POV & Setting:** Clara; Basement Acoustic Darkroom (Day 4, 2:20 AM).
- **Micro-Goal & Conflict:** Racing upstairs sensors, Clara searches the soundproofed room and finds three redundant audio servers, tide charts, and a locked steel cabinet marked *ARCHIVE — DO NOT ERASE*.
- **Value Shift:** Expects murder trophies → Finds what looks like a frantic defense investigation.
- **Cliffhanger / Page-Turn Hook:** Clara pops the cabinet latch with her multi-tool and lifts out a padded manila envelope—just as the steel fire door swings shut and deadbolts from outside.

### Chapter 13
- **POV & Setting:** Clara; Basement Acoustic Darkroom (Day 4, 2:30 AM – 6:00 AM).
- **Micro-Goal & Conflict:** Trapped without cell signal, Clara reads the envelope by flashlight: notarized proof of Julian’s $2.4M embezzlement *plus* unedited 2:14 AM WAV transcripts from the storm night cutting off mid-argument over a boat key.
- **Value Shift:** Believes she’s trapped by a killer → Realizes the room is both a vault and a deliberate puzzle box.
- **Cliffhanger / Page-Turn Hook:** At 6:05 AM, the deadbolt slides open; Julian stands in the frame gripping a heavy brass wrench—and behind him, a pair of muddy women's boots drips onto the tile.

## Midpoint Reversal & Act IIb: The Shattered Frame (Chapters 14–16)

### Chapter 14
- **POV & Setting:** Clara; Basement stairwell & kitchen (Day 4 morning).
- **Micro-Goal & Conflict:** Clara threatens cloud-backed release of the embezzlement files; Julian drops the wrench, confesses the $2.4M theft, and swears he hired Clara specifically because someone is framing him for Evelyn's murder.
- **Value Shift:** Binary hero/villain → Both employer and contractor cornered by an unseen architect *(Midpoint Reversal)*.
- **Cliffhanger / Page-Turn Hook:** Julian points at the dripping boots—they came out of Clara's own rental trunk, planted to implicate her.

### Chapter 15
- **POV & Setting:** Clara & Julian; Study & smart-home server rack (Day 4 midday).
- **Micro-Goal & Conflict:** Forcing Julian to decrypt the full server partition before Cole arrives, Clara plays the unedited 2:14 AM WAV file and hears **Evelyn’s voice** laughing with a man while wiping down an oar.
- **Value Shift:** Chasing a dead woman → Proving Evelyn faked her drowning with an insider accomplice.
- **Cliffhanger / Page-Turn Hook:** A fresh auto-sync pops onto the server from a burner tablet: a spliced distress audio in *Clara’s* voice confessing to helping Julian move a body.

### Chapter 16
- **POV & Setting:** Clara; Cliff stairs toward the boathouse (Day 4 afternoon).
- **Micro-Goal & Conflict:** With Cole’s sirens five minutes out on the ridge road, Clara refuses to flee and bolts down to the boathouse to photograph the staged gear before Cole seizes the property.
- **Value Shift:** Reactive defense → High-risk offensive grab for physical proof *(Act II → Part II Break)*.
- **Cliffhanger / Page-Turn Hook:** Dropping through the boathouse casement, Clara freezes as Evelyn steps from behind the suspended kayak aiming Cole's service pistol: *"Put the phone on the dock, Clara. You weren't supposed to find the dumbwaiter."*

## Part II: The Perspective Flip (Chapters 17–19)

### Chapter 17
- **POV & Setting:** **Evelyn Mercer (3rd Limited Past; Day -24 to Day -21)**; Glass House & Cove.
- **Micro-Goal & Conflict:** Reveals Evelyn uncovering Julian’s $2.4M theft, rejecting his NDA buyout, and recruiting left-handed Deputy Cole Halstead to stage her drowning with tap-water gear and bleach cleanup.
- **Value Shift:** Phantom victim → Calculated frame-job architect.
- **Cliffhanger / Page-Turn Hook:** At 2:14 AM in the sunroom, Evelyn drops her Montblanc pen; Cole retrieves it with his left hand and tosses it back onto the blotter without wiping the nib.

### Chapter 18
- **POV & Setting:** **Evelyn Mercer (3rd Limited Past; Day 0 – Day 4)**; Boathouse loft & dumbwaiter shaft.
- **Micro-Goal & Conflict:** Tracks Evelyn sneaking back from New Brunswick after learning Julian hired an out-of-town fraud investigator instead of local cleaners; watching from the crawlspace, she realizes Clara will break the frame.
- **Value Shift:** Clean frame of Julian → Pivot to framing Clara and Julian in a staged murder-suicide.
- **Cliffhanger / Page-Turn Hook:** Evelyn texts Cole from her burner: *"The cleaner found the shaft. Come early. Bring zip ties."*

### Chapter 19
- **POV & Setting:** Clara (1st Present); Boathouse interior (Day 4 afternoon).
- **Micro-Goal & Conflict:** Zip-tied to a teak piling while Evelyn types Julian’s fake suicide note on a tablet, Clara baits Evelyn into bragging about beating the tide tables while secretly sawing her zip tie against a rusted oyster knife wedged in the bait table.
- **Value Shift:** Helpless captive → Armed and recording Evelyn’s boast over the dock’s active Bluetooth intercom.
- **Cliffhanger / Page-Turn Hook:** Gravel crunches outside—Cole arrives—and Evelyn turns toward the latch right as Clara’s zip tie snaps free.

## Act III: Escalation, Climax & Resolution (Chapters 20–25)

### Chapter 20
- **POV & Setting:** Clara; Boathouse slip & slate cove stairs (Day 4 late afternoon, rising gale).
- **Micro-Goal & Conflict:** Clara slashes Cole’s wrist as he opens the door, dives into the freezing cove channel, and scrambles up the granite stairs to reach the basement drive before Cole locks down the property.
- **Value Shift:** Cornered prisoner → Bleeding fugitive racing inside the kill-box.
- **Cliffhanger / Page-Turn Hook:** Reaching the sunroom glass, Clara sees Julian handcuffed to the radiator—and Cole’s cruiser parked sideways across the flooded causeway throat.

### Chapter 21
- **POV & Setting:** Clara; Pantry & dumbwaiter shaft (Day 4 dusk).
- **Micro-Goal & Conflict:** Slipping through the pantry shaft in fog, Clara tries to reach the basement server while Evelyn’s boots cross the kitchen floor overhead.
- **Value Shift:** Stealth retrieval → Vertical cat-and-mouse inside the house’s hidden shaft.
- **Cliffhanger / Page-Turn Hook:** Clara pulls the pulley pin, dropping the iron counterweight into the basement to bait Cole downward while she climbs *up* into the sunroom.

### Chapter 22
- **POV & Setting:** Clara & Julian; Sunroom & foyer (Day 4 night).
- **Micro-Goal & Conflict:** Clara picks Julian’s cuffs with the shim from her boot heel, but Julian tries to seize the embezzlement ledger to burn it; Clara refuses, keeping all drives and records in her own pack.
- **Value Shift:** Forced allies → Clean break; Clara takes unilateral control of the truth.
- **Cliffhanger / Page-Turn Hook:** Evelyn kills the main breaker from the boathouse subpanel, plunging the house into pitch dark and triggering emergency hurricane shutters on the glass doors.

### Chapter 23
- **POV & Setting:** Clara; Basement Acoustic Darkroom & stairs (Day 4 night).
- **Micro-Goal & Conflict:** In darkness, Clara lures Cole into the darkroom using looped pantry audio, uses the northeast acoustic dead-zone to evade Evelyn’s wrench swing, and slams the steel fire door deadbolt on Cole.
- **Value Shift:** Outgunned → Weaponizes the house’s acoustics and locks to neutralize both threats *(Climax)*.
- **Cliffhanger / Page-Turn Hook:** Evelyn bolts for the foyer skylight hatch with the silver Montblanc and backup drive—right as blue-and-red light bars flare through the coastal fog.

### Chapter 24
- **POV & Setting:** Clara; Gravel forecourt & causeway (Day 4 night, rain).
- **Micro-Goal & Conflict:** Maren—alerted by Clara’s earlier harbor receipt note and Cole’s radio silence—called Maine State Police. Clara hands over the unedited acoustic drive, left-handed pen, salt-free vest, *and* Julian's embezzlement file without sparing anyone.
- **Value Shift:** Framed suspect → Exonerated witness who burns both halves of a rotten marriage.
- **Cliffhanger / Page-Turn Hook:** Sitting in an ambulance bay, a state detective hands Clara a crisp sealed envelope pulled from Julian’s coat: a $10,000 completion check signed not by Julian, but by an anonymous Boston estate firm.

### Chapter 25 (Epilogue / Book 2 Launchpad)
- **POV & Setting:** Clara; Stripped Portland apartment & waterfront diner (Two weeks later).
- **Micro-Goal & Conflict:** Clara clears her $18,000 judgment, receives her restored bond notice, and tries to walk away from off-book cleanups—until Sloane Mercer-Hall (Boston private counsel) slides a brass skeleton key and a photo of a snowed-in Vermont lodge across the booth.
- **Value Shift:** Desperate contractor → Sovereign specialist handed a personal hook she cannot refuse.
- **Cliffhanger / Page-Turn Hook:** Clara flips the Vermont lodge photo over and sees her missing sister’s handwriting in the corner of the guest ledger, dated two days ago.
