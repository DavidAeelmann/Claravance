## 2. Setting & Architectural Blueprint: Barrow Island (`B.I.Q.S.-04`), Lower Narragansett Bay, R.I.
A windswept, treeless twelve-acre hump of black granite and frozen salt marsh in lower Narragansett Bay, exposed to the heavy swell off Block Island Sound. Decommissioned by the State of Rhode Island in **1984** (`Quarantine Station No. 4` / `B.I.Q.S.-04`) and sold to a Providence maritime preservation shell trust (secretly chartered by Kiefer-Hall Fiduciary, renewed in 2014).

- **Marine Perimeter, Tide Clock & Power Grid:**
  - **The Riprap Channel & Boat Slip (4:00 PM Tide Lock):** Rotted oak pilings and a slick granite seawall on the eastern lee. At **4:00 PM**, the south ebb current slams into the submerged granite riprap channel, creating a lethal standing rip that prevents small-craft landing until slack water after dawn. Concrete steps climb from the boat slip through frozen gray slush to the hospital's boarded portico.
  - **Submerged Three-Phase Submarine Power Cable:** Laid covertly across the bay floor during the 2023 infrastructure overhaul (while the six crates were temporarily rotated through Portland's Commercial Street Wharf). Enters the island through a submerged granite conduit beneath the **Disinfection Steam Plant**, stepping down through a 60-cycle oil-cooled transformer to feed uninterrupted commercial three-phase power to Ward C's `4°C` cold-storage vault.
  - **Brick Steam & Pipe Tunnels:** Partially flooded 1890s masonry utility trenches connecting the basements of Ward C, the Administration & Customs House, and the Disinfection Steam Plant (sealed with rusted iron fire dampers and salt-crusted steam pipes).

- **Structure 1: Ward C (Contagious Morgue & `4°C` Cold Vault):**
  - **Exterior & Bulkhead Well:** Three-story 19th-century red-brick contagious hospital wing; arched window openings sealed from the inside with corrugated steel storm shutters bolted into the masonry. On the leeward side sits a recessed granite stairwell leading down twelve steps to sloped iron bulkhead doors (fitted with an exterior steel slide-bolt and a new stainless-steel padlock greased with fresh white lithium lubricant) and an inner oak fire door sheathed in riveted galvanized tin with an antique brass mortise lock (`B.I.Q.S.-04 — WARD C`).
  - **The `4°C` Basement Morgue Vault:** Long whitewashed brick vault lit by low-wattage amber bulkhead cages; concrete drain grate in the center floor; overhead **Halon fire-suppression discharge nozzles** wired to an exterior manual pull-station at the top of the bulkhead stairs.
    - **Four Stainless-Steel Refrigeration Lockers:** Wired into a digital temperature manifold reading **`4°C`** (`39.2°F`) and backed by new industrial compressor units bearing **filed-off serial plates matching the 2023 Commercial Street Wharf salvage crane weights**. **Lockers 1–3** still contain five preserved 1998 steel containment crates (`Crates #01–#05`) housing formalin-cryo biological canisters tagged with passenger donor numbers (`#01-A` through `#11-B`) from the *M/V Northern Cross*. **Locker 4** sits empty, its frost gasket broken less than an hour before Tessa's arrival (`Crate #06` removed).
    - **Center Floor & Autopsy Table:** A freshly pried wooden outer shipping crate stenciled with the faded crest of the **Portland Custom House Wharf** sits on a yellow hydraulic hand-truck beside a forged-steel crowbar and damp pine/cedar slats. On the stainless-steel autopsy table lies Thomas Calder's original black leather **1998 `Calder Maritime & Cargo` manifest binder**, open to the bonded passenger-and-specimen transfer log from the *M/V Northern Cross*.
  - **1890s Brick Autoclave & Saltwater Tidal Drain Culvert:** Built into the rear masonry wall of the Ward C morgue to sterilize and flush nineteenth-century typhus and cholera waste. Behind a rusted cast-iron autoclave clean-out hatch sits a vertical brick cremation/exhaust flue rising into the first-floor Contagious Ward sluice room, intersected at floor level by a sloped granite tidal culvert that empties straight through the seaward riprap (and floods with freezing bay water as the 4:00 PM tide turns).
  - **Upper Contagious Ward (Floors 1–3):** Peeling lead paint, iron diagnostic beds, shuttered window slits offering tiny sightlines toward the boat slip and Administration House, and an interior fire stair leading down to the utility tunnel junction.

- **Structure 2: The Administration & Customs House:**
  - **Layout:** Two-story mansard-roofed brick headquarters fifty paces north of Ward C, overlooking the boat slip and granite breakwater.
  - **The Burn-and-Transfer Room (First-Floor Chief Surgeon's Office):** Victor Garroway's temporary command post, warmed by a portable kerosene space heater and thick with **Latakia Turkish pipe tobacco** smoke. Contains a cast-iron drum stove and heavy cross-cut paper shredder where Garroway and Margot Kiefer-Hall are purging three caches of physical records:
    1. The original **1998 Harbor Mutual `$24,000,000` marine hull-and-loss underwriting file** on the scuttled research vessel *M/V Northern Cross*.
    2. The **2023 Commercial Street Wharf internal arson and lockbox-framing dossier** used to strip Tessa's license and impose the `$42,000` judgment.
    3. The **2014 Portland death certificate and toxicology sign-off** on Thomas Calder's "acute coronary"—co-signed by a consulting medical examiner on Kiefer-Hall Fiduciary's State Street retainer (`Account #884-19B`) after Thomas was poisoned with concentrated `CutlerCor`.
  - **Communications & Escrow Terminal:** Encrypted satellite router, marine VHF base station, a burner phone linking Garroway to his two-man fake U.S. Marshals extraction team in Portland, Maine, and Margot's portable banking token for **State Street Escrow Account `#884-19B`**.

- **Structure 3: The Disinfection Steam Plant & Coal Pier:**
  - **The Steam Plant:** Cavernous corrugated-iron and brick boiler house on the southern deep-water cove. Houses silent nineteenth-century coal disinfection retorts, rusted overhead iron catwalks (which amplify metallic footsteps), and the **modern 3-phase submarine cable transformer box** and master breaker switchgear feeding Ward C and the Administration House.
  - **The Coal Pier & Slipway:** Heavy granite-and-timber pier extending into deep water on the lee of the breakwater, equipped with an iced-over iron winch slipway. Moored alongside is Victor Garroway's private 38-foot twin-diesel cabin launch (*Harbor Bell*), its aft deck tarped over **Refrigerated Steel Containment Crate `#06` (`PASSENGER / DONOR #12-C: CALDER, ELENA M.`)**, weighted with iron chain links for a midnight dump into the 200-foot trench off Block Island Sound.

- **Structure 4: The North Ledge Signal Tower (Decommissioned Lighthouse):**
  - **Layout:** Octagonal granite tower perched on the highest northern rock ledge above the crashing surf, connected to the Steam Plant by an exposed gravel causeway swept by freezing sleet.
  - **The Microwave Repeater & Distress Array:** Although the Fresnel lamp was removed in 1984, the steel gallery at the top of the spiral iron stairs still houses an automated **U.S. Coast Guard VHF/microwave marine weather and emergency distress repeater** wired into a solar/battery trickle bank—capable of punching a hardwired emergency telemetry packet and voice carrier to **USCG Sector Southeastern New England (Woods Hole / Point Judith)** even when Garroway's portable cell jammer blankets the lower buildings.

## 3. Cast Dossiers, Motives & Physical/Handedness Anchors

1. **Tessa Calder (31 — Protagonist, 100% 1st-Person Present POV):**
   - **Physical & Forensic Anchors:** Right-handed; thin pink scar on left wrist (Salt Point); tight pink scar across right index knuckle (Cragmont Lodge, sutures removed Dec 13). Drives a 2014 silver Honda Civic (parked at Galilee landing).
   - **Core Stakes & Drive:** Came to Barrow Island believing she was exposing Victor Garroway's 2023 Portland wharf framing and clearing her father's 1998 maritime surveying stamp. Within minutes of being locked in the `4°C` vault, she discovers the red-circled name on the 1998 passenger-and-specimen log is her own mother—**`CALDER, ELENA M. — SPECIMEN CRATE 06`**—and that Garroway is hours away from dumping her mother's preserved heart into the Atlantic while sending a fake federal transfer team to Portland to murder Nina.

2. **Nina Calder (27 — Tessa's Younger Sister, in Portland, Maine):**
   - **Physical & Forensic Anchors:** **Left-handed** (back-slanted cursive with downward-tailed lowercase `m`; uses `Noodler's Prussian Blue` ink); taped ribs from Cragmont Ridge; childhood scar on right collarbone.
   - **Role & Vulnerability in Book 3:** Physically located inside Tessa's third-floor apartment on the **Eastern Promenade in Portland, Maine**, under 24-hour protection by two U.S. Marshals. In early 2020, Nina took her father's rusted steel lockbox from Tessa's spare-room bankers' boxes to Burlington out of sentimentality; when she uncovered the *CutlerCor* deaths in October 2020 and opened the lockbox, she saw Victor Garroway's 1998 signature and realized Tessa had just been hired at Harbor Mutual by the very man who owned the 1998 Barrow Island crates. In Act II of Book 3, Garroway dispatches a two-man hit team to Portland with a forged District of Maine U.S. Marshals nocturnal witness-relocation order (`Form USM-129`) to walk Nina out of Tessa's apartment into an ambush before dawn.

3. **Victor Garroway (61 — Chief Marine Underwriter, Harbor Mutual Insurance):**
   - **Physical & Forensic Anchors:** **Right-handed** (signs with a wide gold-nibbed Montblanc Diplomat in thick black fountain ink); silver hair combed straight back, tailored navy cashmere overcoat, brass buttonhole lapel pin; perpetually smokes **sweet, cloying Latakia Turkish pipe tobacco** from a briar pipe (leaving white ash and burnt-latakia resin wherever he stands).
   - **Motive & History:** Architect of the "Shield" pillar of the Triad Syndicate.
     - **1998:** Underwrote the `$24,000,000` marine hull-and-loss policy on the chartered hospital vessel *M/V Northern Cross*; recruited his Portland friend Thomas Calder by offering Thomas's dying wife Elena a spot in Cutler Biotech's offshore cardiac trial in exchange for Thomas's `C.M.C.` survey stamp; coerced Thomas into 16 years of silence by threatening to frame him for the Block Island sinking and put 4-year-old Tessa and newborn Nina into foster care.
     - **2014:** When the 30-year Barrow Island lease expired and Thomas refused to sign a backdated decommissioning certificate, Garroway and Hollis Greer murdered Thomas in Portland with an overdose of `CutlerCor` (ruled a natural coronary) and raided Thomas's Custom House Wharf office—recovering the black leather 1998 `C.M.C.` binder, but missing the torn carbon copy and the spare `WARD C` brass key hidden in Tessa's apartment.
     - **2023:** When the FDA required original 1998 baseline tissue-stability recertification for *CutlerCor*, Garroway rotated the six Barrow Island crates through a Commercial Street Wharf warehouse in Portland while upgrading Ward C's compressors and laying the 3-phase submarine cable. When Tessa flagged the forged "cotton yarn" crane weights and the `B.I.Q.S.-04` stamp, Garroway burned the warehouse and framed Tessa for lockbox tampering rather than killing her—knowing a murdered Calder daughter would trigger a federal audit of her desk, whereas a bankrupt, discredited Tessa in Portland served as stationary bait if Nina ever surfaced with the missing 1998 carbon sheet.
     - **December 16, 2026:** Tipped off by Margot Kiefer-Hall that AUSA Halvorsen has the *Caretaker's Ledger* (`B.I.Q.S.-04 / C.M.C. — AUDIT 1998`), Garroway is on Barrow Island to dump `Crate #06` (`ELENA M. CALDER`) at midnight, burn the 1998/2023 paper trail, drain Margot's State Street escrow reserve into his Cayman account, and eliminate both Calder sisters.

4. **Conrad Vale (56 — Harbor Mutual Chief Salvage Fixer / Former USCG Marine Inspector):**
   - **Physical & Forensic Anchors:** **Right-handed**; burly, close-cropped iron-gray hair, salt-scarred cheek, wears a dark oilskin trench coat over men's leather dress boots fitted with a **custom orthopedic steel toe-tap and raised sole on the right boot** (leaves a crisp crescent toe-tap and sharp heel strike in slush and a distinct metallic *tick-thud* on iron catwalks and granite stairs—the result of a crushed right forefoot when a davit cable snapped aboard the *M/V Northern Cross* during the November 1998 Block Island scuttling). Smells of **white lithium grease, diesel bilge, and menthol nasal spray**.
   - **Motive & Role:** Garroway's long-time maritime enforcer and the deck officer who scuttled the *Northern Cross* in 1998 and torched the Commercial Street Wharf warehouse in 2023. At the end of Chapter 1, Vale is the man who wheeled the hydraulic hand-truck up from the boat slip, spotted the unlatched Ward C bulkhead, snapped off the vault breaker, and bolted Tessa inside the `4°C` morgue.

5. **Margot Kiefer-Hall (44 — Managing Partner, Kiefer-Hall Fiduciary, Boston):**
   - **Physical & Forensic Anchors:** **Right-handed**; silver-streaked bob, camel-hair overcoat stained with salt spray at the hem, smells of espresso and dry archival bond paper.
   - **Motive & Role:** Architect of the "Capital" pillar of the Triad Syndicate (`State Street Escrow Account #884-19B`). Out on a `$500,000` federal surety bond after Book 2, Margot skipped bail and rode Garroway's private launch to Barrow Island because the 1998 *Northern Cross* offshore trust charter was drafted by Kiefer-Hall Fiduciary: if Ward C's biological specimens and the 1998 `C.M.C.` binder fall into federal hands, her 2020 fraud charge escalates into nine counts of 1998 maritime murder and capital conspiracy. She is desperate to oversee the destruction of `Crate #06` and the 1998 binder—unaware that Garroway has already queued an offshore wire to empty her State Street escrow reserve and frame her as the sole architect of the 1998 trial and 2023 Portland wharf fire.

6. **AUSA Nora Halvorsen (48 — Assistant U.S. Attorney, District of Vermont / Federal Task Force):**
   - **Role:** Federal prosecutor who took custody of the original *Caretaker's Ledger* at St. Johnsbury ER at the end of Book 2 and arranged the two-Marshal detail outside Tessa's Portland apartment. Reachable only if Tessa can bypass Garroway's cell jammer via the North Ledge Coast Guard repeater or customs land-trunk relay before the fake U.S. Marshals team breaches Tessa's Portland apartment.

7. **Thomas Calder (Deceased 2014, Age 52) & Elena M. Calder (Deceased Nov 1998, Age 29) — Posthumous Anchors:**
   - **Elena M. Calder:** Tessa and Nina's mother (`PASSENGER / DONOR #12-C`). Suffered fatal postpartum ion-channel cardiomyopathy after delivering Nina in November 1998. Lured aboard the *M/V Northern Cross* by Garroway's promise of a clinical trial; died of a `CutlerCor` precursor QT-interval spike while her unique genetic mutation gave Cutler Biotech the biological baseline for their `$38,000,000` patent. Her harvested heart and formalin-cryo myocardial tissue blocks have been stored at `4°C` inside **Locker 4 / Specimen Crate `#06`** on Barrow Island for 28 years.
   - **Thomas Calder:** Owner of `Calder Maritime & Cargo` on Custom House Wharf, Portland. Coerced into silence in November 1998 after discovering his own wife's name on the specimen offload manifest he had already stamped (`C.M.C. — AUDIT 1998`). Secretly preserved the torn carbon copy and spare `WARD C` key in a rusted steel lockbox; murdered in 2014 by Garroway and Hollis Greer with concentrated `CutlerCor` when he refused to recertify the Barrow Island lease.
