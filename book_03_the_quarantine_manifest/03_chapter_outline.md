# 03 — Fair-Play Clue Matrix & 23-Chapter Blueprint (*The Quarantine Manifest* v2)

**Target Manuscript Length:** ~42,000–48,000 words across **23 chapters** (~1,750–2,200 words per chapter across 3 discrete ~600–750-word scene beats; **Chapter 1** locked verbatim at 1,275 words from Book 2's back-matter preview).
**POV & Tense Architecture:**
- **Chapters 1–23:** **100% Tessa Calder — Strict 1st-Person Present Tense (`I`)**.
- **Zero Third-Person Flashback Chapters:** All of 1998, 2014, and 2023 is uncovered on the page through documents, objects, and confrontation.
- **Containment:** **100% Barrow Island.** Nina Calder is safe with AUSA Nora Halvorsen and is **never on-page until Chapter 23**. Her absence creates intellectual pressure rather than physical danger.

**MASTER TIDE & WEATHER TABLE (Block Island Sound, Dec 16–17, 2026)**
- **Low water `3:58 PM`:** The flood begins and the riprap channel becomes a 3-knot rip by **`4:05 PM`**.
- **High water `10:11 PM`:** Ward C's tidal culvert is fully charged from **`9:30 PM` to `12:15 AM`**.
- **Ebb running hard from `12:30 AM`:** Matches Garroway's chart (`"00:30 EBB — 200 FT"`).
- **Weather & Sunrise:** Sleet squall from **`11:00 PM` to `2:00 AM`**. Sunrise **`7:08 AM`**.

## 2. 23-Chapter Beat-by-Beat Blueprint

### Act I: The Vault and the Tide Clock (Chapters 1–6)

#### Chapter 1 (LOCKED VERBATIM — 1,275 Words)
- **POV & Tense:** Tessa Calder (1st-Person Present).
- **Setting & Timestamp:** Narragansett mail skiff, Barrow Island breakwater, Ward C bulkhead stairwell, `4°C` vault (`Wed Dec 16, 3:20–3:35 PM`).
- **Beat:** Tessa lands with the skiff, her father's key and the 1998 carbon sheet, and a 40-minute window before the tide turns. Plants **Clues 1–4 and 9**.
- **Hook:** The lights die, the bulkhead doors slam, and a slide-bolt grates shut.

#### Chapter 2 — Ward C Vault
- **Timestamp:** `3:35–3:44 PM`.
- **Beat:** By flashlight she photographs the circled line: her mother's name (`CALDER, ELENA M. — SPECIMEN CRATE 06`), countersigned by Garroway, dated Nov 19, 1998. She hasn't turned the page yet. Lockers 1–3 hold Crates #01–#05; Locker 4 is empty, its gasket broken within the hour. Under the loupe, the compressor lot code matches 2023. She registers the autoclave hatch and the kelp smell from the culvert, and notices the **`"M"`** stamped on her key without yet understanding it.
- **Hook:** *Tick-thud* at the top of the steps, glass snaps in the red box, and the nozzles hiss. It's a **CO₂ total-flooding system**, retrofitted in 2023.

#### Chapter 3 — Autoclave and Flue
- **Timestamp:** `3:44–3:53 PM`.
- **Beat:** CO₂ fills the vault from the floor up. Holding her breath high, she bags the binder, takes the crowbar from the crate, and snaps the 1890s dog latch. She climbs above the gas line because the gas pools low; rising is survival. Seawater starts gurgling into the culvert below. Her right-knuckle scar splits.
- **Hook:** The mortar shelf shears under her left boot above the flooding culvert.

#### Chapter 4 — Sluice Room and East Windows
- **Timestamp:** `3:53–4:05 PM`.
- **Beat:** She hauls herself through by her scarred left wrist. At `3:56 PM` she watches through a shutter gap as Vale (*tick-thud* on the pier steps) pays off the skiff. The rip closes the channel (`4:05 PM`). Her phone shows `NO SERVICE`; there's a jammer.
- **Hook:** Under the Customs House portico stand Garroway with his pipe and Margot Kiefer-Hall, who has skipped bail.

#### Chapter 5 — Second-Floor Gallery
- **Timestamp:** `4:05–4:35 PM`.
- **Beat:** At `4:12 PM` Vale vents the vault and goes down in a respirator. Through the radiator chase, Tessa hears him on VHF Channel 72: the lock was turned clean, with no scratches, so whoever did it had a key, and only one key was ever missing: *"It's Calder's daughter."* That tells Tessa they know her father's key exists.
- **Hook:** His flashlight goes up the flue, and the *tick-thud* starts up the fire stairs.

#### Chapter 6 — Pipe Trench and Steam Tunnel
- **Timestamp:** `4:35–5:15 PM` (nightfall).
- **Beat:** A counterweighted stretcher dolly masks her movement. In the tunnel she touches the warm armored cable and feels the 60-cycle hum; it runs south to the Steam Plant and branches north. Through a vent grate she maps all four buildings and the North Ledge tower with its USCG dish.
- **Hook:** From the pier above, Garroway says: *"Crate Six stays chained to the stern winch till the ebb. Two hundred feet, Conrad."* Tessa supplies the name he doesn't say. *(End of Act I)*

### Act II: Pathology, Paperwork, and a Poisonous Alliance (Chapters 7–15)

#### Chapter 7 — *Harbor Bell*
- **Timestamp:** `5:15–5:56 PM`.
- **Beat:** Under a tarp on the launch's stern sits `Crate #06`, chained and locked with a seven-pin tubular lock. Her multi-tool can't defeat it, and the inability matters later. Its brass plate reads `NORTHERN CROSS — SPECIMEN 06 / DONOR #12-C (CALDER, E.M.)`. In the wheelhouse she finds the bathymetry chart marked `"00:30 EBB — 200 FT"`; the kill-switch fob has been pulled.
- **Hook:** The Steam Plant door bangs open and a halogen searchlight sweeps toward the stern.

#### Chapter 8 — Steam Plant
- **Timestamp:** `5:56–6:45 PM`.
- **Beat:** From behind Retort #2 she studies Vale: valves, seals, breaker plates, always in the same order. On the switchgear: `MAIN BAY FEED 480V`; `WARD C VAULT & HIGH-TEMP ALARM`; `ADMINISTRATION / CUSTOMS HOUSE`. She leaves the breakers alone for now.
- **Hook:** A work order in a plastic sleeve: the cable was billed to Commercial Street Wharf Warehouse #4 and countersigned by Garroway on Oct 18, 2023, 48 hours before the fire.

#### Chapter 9 — Coal Loft Audit (The Emotional Core)
- **Timestamp:** `6:45–7:30 PM`.
- **Beat:** She goes through the binder page by page. Twelve patients boarded at Providence. Nine died of QT spikes. Three are marked **`"DISCHARGED — K-H TRUST ANNUITY"`**. The casualty report records C. Vale's crushed forefoot. Thomas's certification predates the deaths: he knowingly signed false papers to get Elena aboard. Then, beside `Crate 06`, written later in his square print: **`I DID NOT KNOW WHAT WAS IN 06.`** The assay columns and sample codes defeat her. She needs Nina.
- **Hook:** The Customs House chimney pours black smoke from burning bond paper.

#### Chapter 10 — North Ledge and the Customs House Cellar
- **Timestamp:** `7:30–8:15 PM`.
- **Beat:** At the repeater she finds 24V trickle power but no keying jumper; she needs a VHF handset. Then the cellar grate: Latakia smoke and burning paper.
- **Hook:** Margot is shouting at Garroway, demanding the file-room key. He refuses.

#### Chapter 11 — File Room
- **Timestamp:** `8:15–9:00 PM`.
- **Beat:** Her father's key turns the oak mortise; the **`"M"`** means master (**Clue #9**). She works through three caches:
  1. The `CutlerCor` revenue ledger, about **`$1B`** cumulative (`$38M` was only the family's voting block). Every recertification was benchmarked against the *Northern Cross* cohort. The evidence was the product.
  2. The 2023 rotation and arson dossier: her framing, and duplicate blocks shipped to a contract lab for FDA recertification, listed by lot code only.
  3. The 1998 Kiefer-Hall charter instruments. From what she overheard, she understands these are what Margot came for.
- **Hook:** A folder stamped `B.I.Q.S.-04 — COLD STORAGE UPGRADE (NOV 2014)`.

#### Chapter 12 — The Unsigned Line
- **Timestamp:** `9:00–9:40 PM`.
- **Beat:** The work order is dated seven days before her father's "coronary," with his decommissioning certificate attached and **unsigned**. She deduces the refusal and the murder, and suspects the method (`CutlerCor`, as with Arthur in Book 2) but can't prove it. On Garroway's terminal, a live Cayman wire is draining Escrow `#884-19B`. She photographs it and takes the VHF handset.
- **Hook:** A reflection in the glass, and Margot's .380 at her neck.

#### Chapter 13 — The Truce
- **Timestamp:** `9:40–10:05 PM`.
- **Beat:** Tessa doesn't beg. She shows Margot the wire. Margot realizes Garroway suspects her and is cutting her loose. Tessa keeps the charter instruments in her own duffel, which is her only leverage. They agree a truce until dawn: Margot won't shoot her, and Tessa won't burn the papers. Footsteps on the gravel. Tessa goes out the sash window with the binder, the folders, and the handset.
- **Hook:** Garroway into the dark: *"Halvorsen's keeping Nina in a nice place, Tessa. I know which one."*

#### Chapter 14 — Third-Floor Loft
- **Timestamp:** `10:05–10:25 PM`.
- **Beat:** She can't verify the threat. Then she reasons it through: if he knew, he'd act on it, not announce it. It's a bluff, though she isn't certain. She seals the evidence in the oilskin liner and **copies the lab lot codes onto her forearm in pen**, in case she loses the paper. She still can't decode them.
- **Hook:** From the courtyard: *"Your father signed before anyone died. Bring the binder to the pier by eleven."*

#### Chapter 15 — The Armed Gift
- **Timestamp:** `10:25–10:45 PM`.
- **Beat:** The line lands because it's true. She sets her plan. Meeting Margot at the service stair, she **deliberately hands over the charter instruments** but **keeps the annuity pages**, which carry Margot's signature. She knows Margot will defect the moment she's aboard the launch. Tessa is now using people the way they use people, and she notices it.
- **Hook:** The bulkhead doors clang open behind her. It's Vale's `10:45 PM` tunnel check, *tick-thud*. *(End of Act II)*

### Act III: The Purge and the Reckoning (Chapters 16–23)

#### Chapter 16 — Switchgear
- **Timestamp:** `10:45–11:10 PM`.
- **Beat:** She outruns Vale through the tunnel and wedges the damper. She throws the Customs House breaker and trips the vault high-temperature alarm; a klaxon goes off. Over the radio, Garroway sends Vale to the breaker while he and Margot go to the launch.
- **Hook:** The damper pin pops and Vale walks in with his flashlight off. He knows the room.

#### Chapter 17 — Catwalks
- **Timestamp:** `11:10–11:35 PM`.
- **Beat:** She predicts his order: breaker first, then the upper isolation valve. She waits at the valve, opens the petcock in his face, drops the hatch, and bars it. She takes his tubular key ring as he lunges.
- **Hook:** The *Harbor Bell*'s diesels start.

#### Chapter 18 — `Crate #06`
- **Timestamp:** `11:35–11:52 PM`.
- **Beat:** In the wheelhouse, Garroway finds the charter instruments in Margot's coat. Their partnership detonates the way Tessa intended. On the aft deck, Vale's key opens the crate: rows of blocks, slides, and cryovials. From slot `12-C` she lifts a small cold cassette: **`CALDER E.M. / 12-C / LV`**.
- **Hook:** A shot inside the wheelhouse. Garroway comes out with his .357, and Margot is down behind him, hit in the shoulder.

#### Chapter 19 — The Culvert
- **Timestamp:** `11:52 PM–12:10 AM` (just past high-water slack; culvert fully charged).
- **Beat:** Tessa runs. Garroway follows her to the Ward C courtyard. She kicks the wedged culvert grate free and the charged culvert dumps across the granite apron, taking his feet out from under him. The revolver goes into the riprap.
- **Hook:** Both of them are on their knees in seawater, three meters apart, and he doesn't run.

#### Chapter 20 — The Audit
- **Timestamp:** `12:10–12:25 AM`, Thu Dec 17.
- **Beat:** This is the face-to-face she's been building toward. She lays out 2014: the date, the blank line. He doesn't deny it, and that is the confession. *"Your father signed before anyone died."* *"He signed for cargo."* She reads him the annuity signatures, which also sink Margot. He wants her to understand him. She doesn't.
- **Hook:** The cutter isn't coming yet, and Garroway is up and running for the launch.

#### Chapter 21 — North Ledge
- **Timestamp:** `12:25–12:42 AM`.
- **Beat:** She climbs 80 feet, bridges the keying relay with the handset, and gets 15 ragged seconds through to USCG Sector Southeastern New England and Halvorsen's watch desk before the relay shorts.
- **Hook:** Below, the *Harbor Bell*'s searchlight is on Tessa's duffel, which she left on the pier when she ran. Garroway is going back for the binder.

#### Chapter 22 — The Choice
- **Timestamp:** `12:42–1:10 AM` (ebb running hard).
- **Beat:** She reaches the pier first. The launch reverses into the pilings and a parted mooring line whips across the apron. The duffel with the binder slides toward the water on one side; the cassette, knocked from her pocket, skates across the iced slipway on the other. She can reach one. **She saves the binder.** The cassette goes into the black water. Garroway clears the pier and sees the cutter's lights. He cuts `Crate #06` loose over the trench, so the purge succeeds, then misjudges the ebb rip and broaches on the shoal.
- **Hook:** Tessa, soaked, holds the binder as searchlights cross the wreck and the east starts to gray.

#### Chapter 23 — Resolution
- **Dawn on the island (`7:08 AM` sunrise):** Vale is still barred in the loft. Margot is alive, cuffed on a backboard. Federal teams seal Crates `#01–#05`. Locker 4 is logged.
- **One week later:** Halvorsen confirms the Congress Street threat was a bluff. **Nina decodes the lot codes Tessa wrote on her arm:** one duplicate block, **`CALDER E.M. / 12-C / LV-D2`**, held since 2023 at a contract lab under **GLP archive retention**. The syndicate's own record-keeping preserved her. Garroway, Vale, and Margot are charged. Margot's deal partly survives, cut down by the annuity signatures. The `$42,000` judgment is vacated. Thomas's record is **corrected**, not cleared.
- **Dec 23, Eastern Promenade kitchen:** An unopened envelope from Harbor Mutual's counsel sits on the counter. The burial is deferred, because Elena is evidence until trial. Nina says they'll take her out past Cape Elizabeth when it's over. Tessa reads the **amended death certificate** the way she reads everything: weight, date, signature, cause. It's the first true record in three books. She sets her father's key beside it.
